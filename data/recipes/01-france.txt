# Steak frites | Steak-frites | 牛排配薯条
@ Beef | France | 40 | fr:Steak-frites | Classic,Meat
- Sirloin Steak: 720g
- Floury Potatoes: 1kg
- Sunflower Oil: For frying
- Butter: 30g
- Shallots: 1
- Parsley: 2 tbs chopped
- Salt: to taste
- Black Pepper: to taste
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 1cm sticks. Rinse in cold water and dry well with a clean tea towel.
2. Heat the oil to 160C and fry the chips in batches for 6 minutes until soft but not coloured. Drain on kitchen paper.
3. Take the steaks out of the fridge 15 minutes before cooking. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Raise the oil to 180C and fry the chips again for 3 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain and salt.
5. Heat a heavy frying pan until very hot, add a knob of butter and cook the steaks for 2 to 3 minutes on each side for medium-rare. Rest for 3 minutes.
6. Melt the rest of the butter in the pan with the finely chopped shallot and parsley, spoon over the steaks and serve with the chips.

# Blanquette de veau | Blanquette de veau | 法式白汁炖小牛肉
@ Beef | France | 120 | fr:Blanquette de veau | Classic,Stew
- Veal: 1kg
- Carrots: 3
- Onion: 1
- Cloves: 2
- Bouquet Garni: 1
- Mushrooms: 200g
- Butter: 40g
- Plain Flour: 40g
- Creme Fraiche: 200ml
- Egg Yolks: 1
- Lemon: 1/2
- Rice: 300g
1. Put the veal in a large pot, cover with cold water and bring to the boil. Skim off the foam.
2. Add the carrots cut in chunks, the onion studded with the cloves and the bouquet garni. Simmer gently for 1 hour 30 minutes.
3. Cook the rice. Fry the quartered mushrooms in a little butter for 5 minutes.
4. Lift out the meat and carrots and keep warm. Strain and keep 750ml of the cooking stock.
5. Melt the butter in the pot, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute, then whisk in the stock and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.
6. Off the heat, mix the crème fraîche with the egg yolk and lemon juice, stir into the sauce, add the meat, carrots and mushrooms and warm through without boiling. Serve with the rice.

# Hachis parmentier | Hachis parmentier | 法式牧羊人派
@ Beef | France | 60 | fr:Hachis parmentier | Classic,Bake
- Floury Potatoes: 1kg
- Minced Beef: 600g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Milk: 200ml
- Butter: 50g
- Gruyère: 80g
- Nutmeg: 1 pinch
- Salt: to taste
- Black Pepper: to taste
1. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water for 20 minutes until tender.
2. Meanwhile fry the chopped onions in a little butter for 5 minutes, add the garlic and the minced beef and cook for 8 minutes, breaking it up. Stir in the tomato purée and season.
3. Mash the potatoes with the hot milk, the butter and the nutmeg. Season.
4. Heat the oven to 200C. Spread the meat in a baking dish, cover with the mash and sprinkle with the grated cheese.
5. Bake for 20 minutes until golden.

# Quiche lorraine | Quiche lorraine | 洛林乳蛋饼
@ Pork | France | 50 | fr:Quiche lorraine | Classic,Tart
- Shortcrust Pastry: 1 sheet
- Bacon Lardon: 200g
- Eggs: 3
- Double Cream: 200ml
- Milk: 100ml
- Gruyère: 60g
- Nutmeg: 1 pinch
- Black Pepper: to taste
- Lettuce: 1
- Vinaigrette Dressing: 3 tbs
1. Heat the oven to 200C. Line a tart tin with the pastry and prick the base with a fork.
2. Fry the lardons in a dry pan for 5 minutes and drain.
3. Whisk the eggs with the cream, milk, nutmeg and pepper.
4. Scatter the lardons and grated cheese over the pastry and pour over the egg mixture.
5. Bake for 30 minutes until set and golden. Serve warm with a green salad dressed with vinaigrette.

# Croque-monsieur | Croque-monsieur | 法式火腿奶酪烤三明治
@ Pork | France | 20 | fr:Croque-monsieur | Quick,Sandwich
- White Bread: 8 slices
- Ham: 4 slices
- Gruyère: 150g
- Butter: 30g
- Milk: 250ml
- Plain Flour: 20g
- Nutmeg: 1 pinch
- Lettuce: 1
1. Make a quick béchamel: melt half the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk and cook for 3 minutes until thick. Season with nutmeg.
2. Heat the oven grill. Butter the bread on one side.
3. Lay 4 slices buttered side down, spread with a little béchamel, add a slice of ham and some cheese, then close with the other slices, buttered side up.
4. Spread the rest of the béchamel on top and cover with the remaining cheese.
5. Grill for 8 minutes until bubbling and golden. Serve with a green salad.

# Roast chicken with potatoes | Poulet rôti et pommes de terre | 烤鸡配土豆
@ Chicken | France | 90 | fr:Poulet rôti | Classic,Roast
- Chicken: 1.5kg
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Thyme: 4 sprigs
- Butter: 30g
- Olive Oil: 3 tbs
- Lemon: 1
- Salt: to taste
- Black Pepper: to taste
1. Heat the oven to 210C. Rub the chicken with the soft butter, salt and pepper and put the halved lemon and two thyme sprigs inside.
2. Cut the potatoes into chunks and toss with the olive oil, the unpeeled garlic cloves, thyme, salt and pepper.
3. Put the chicken in a roasting tin with the potatoes around it.
4. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes, basting twice, until the juices run clear.
5. Rest the chicken for 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the potatoes and the cooking juices.

# Beef and carrot stew | Bœuf carottes | 胡萝卜炖牛肉
@ Beef | France | 150 | fr:Bœuf mode | Stew,Classic
- Beef: 1kg
- Carrots: 1kg
- Onions: 2
- Bacon Lardon: 100g
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Dry White Wine: 250ml
- Beef Stock: 500ml
- Bouquet Garni: 1
- Olive Oil: 2 tbs
- Potatoes: 600g
1. Brown the beef in the oil in a casserole in batches. Set aside.
2. Fry the lardons and sliced onions for 5 minutes, then return the beef, sprinkle with flour and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine and stock, add the bouquet garni, cover and simmer gently for 1 hour.
4. Add the carrots cut into thick rounds and simmer for 1 hour more until the beef is very tender.

# Pot-au-feu | Pot-au-feu | 法式蔬菜炖牛肉
@ Beef | France | 210 | fr:Pot-au-feu | Classic,Stew,Soup
- Beef Shin: 1.2kg
- Carrots: 6
- Leek: 2
- Turnips: 4
- Potatoes: 600g
- Onion: 1
- Cloves: 3
- Bouquet Garni: 1
- Peppercorns: 1 tsp
- Coarse Salt: 1 tbs
- Dijon Mustard: to serve
1. Put the beef in a large pot, cover with 4 litres of cold water and bring slowly to the boil. Skim well.
2. Add the onion studded with cloves, the bouquet garni, peppercorns and salt. Simmer gently for 2 hours 30 minutes.
3. Add the carrots, leeks and turnips and simmer for 40 minutes more.
4. Cook the potatoes in a ladle of the broth for 25 minutes.
5. Serve the broth first, then the sliced meat and vegetables with mustard and coarse salt.

- Tuna: 300g
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Sweetcorn: 150g
- Onion: 1
- Creme Fraiche: 100ml
- Mozzarella: 125g
- Cheddar Cheese: 80g
1. Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet says.
2. Soften the chopped onion in a little oil for 5 minutes, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in the crème fraîche.
3. Heat the oven to 200C. Mix the pasta with the sauce, the tuna and the sweetcorn.
4. Put in a dish, top with the torn mozzarella and grated cheddar and bake for 15 minutes.

- Leek: 3
- Potatoes: 600g
- Onion: 1
- Butter: 30g
- Vegetable Stock: 1.2 litres
- Creme Fraiche: 100ml
- Chives: 2 tbs chopped
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Soften the sliced leeks and onion in the butter for 10 minutes without colouring.
2. Add the diced potatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Blend until smooth, stir in the crème fraîche and season.
4. Sprinkle with chives and serve with crusty bread.

# Buckwheat galettes with ham, egg and cheese | Galettes complètes | 布列塔尼荞麦可丽饼
@ Pork | France | 40 | File:Galette de sarrasin complète bretonne.jpg | Brittany
- Buckwheat: 250g
- Eggs: 5
- Water: 500ml
- Salted Butter: 50g
- Ham: 4 slices
- Emmentaler Cheese: 150g
- Lettuce: 1
1. Whisk the buckwheat flour with 1 egg, the water and a pinch of salt. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Heat a large frying pan, butter it and pour in a ladle of batter, swirling to cover the pan. Cook for 2 minutes.
3. Turn the galette, sprinkle with cheese, add a slice of ham and crack an egg in the middle.
4. Cook for 3 minutes until the white is set, fold the edges over in a square and serve with salad.

# Courgette and goat's cheese gratin | Gratin de courgettes au chèvre | 山羊奶酪焗西葫芦
@ Vegetarian | France | 45 | File:Préparation gratin de courgettes-tomates (017) - sortie du four.jpg | Bake,Vegetarian
- Courgettes: 4
- Goats Cheese: 150g
- Eggs: 3
- Creme Fraiche: 200ml
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Thyme: 1 tsp
- Rice: 250g
1. Heat the oven to 190C. Slice the courgettes and fry them with the garlic in a little oil for 8 minutes.
2. Beat the eggs with the crème fraîche, thyme, salt and pepper.
3. Put the courgettes in a dish, crumble over the goat's cheese and pour over the egg mixture.
4. Bake for 25 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Chicken and leek pie | Tourte poulet poireaux | 鸡肉韭葱派
@ Chicken | France | 55 | File:Chicken & leek pie with mushroom - The Kew Greenhouse Cafe 2025-05-14.jpg | Pie,Family
- Chicken Breasts: 3
- Leek: 3
- Butter: 30g
- Plain Flour: 2 tbs
- Chicken Stock: 300ml
- Creme Fraiche: 150ml
- Puff Pastry: 1 sheet
- Egg: 1
- Lettuce: 1
1. Cut the chicken into cubes and brown in the butter for 5 minutes. Add the sliced leeks and cook for 8 minutes.
2. Sprinkle with flour, stir, then add the stock and crème fraîche and simmer for 5 minutes. Season.
3. Heat the oven to 200C. Pour into a pie dish and cover with the pastry, pressing the edges.
4. Brush with beaten egg and bake for 25 minutes until golden. Serve with salad.
