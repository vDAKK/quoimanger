# Cheese and ham omelette with salad | Omelette jambon fromage | 火腿奶酪煎蛋卷
@ Pork | France | 15 | en:Omelette | Quick,Eggs
- Eggs: 10
- Ham: 150g
- Gruyère: 80g
- Butter: 20g
- Lettuce: 1
- Vinaigrette Dressing: 3 tbs
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.
2. Melt a little butter in a frying pan, pour in a quarter of the eggs and stir gently for 1 minute.
3. Add some diced ham and cheese, fold and slide onto a plate. Repeat.
4. Serve with the dressed salad and bread.

# Pasta with creamy mushrooms | Pâtes crémeuses aux champignons | 奶油蘑菇意面
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 20 | File:Creamy Mushroom Pasta.jpg | Quick,Pasta,Vegetarian
- Tagliatelle: 400g
- Mushrooms: 400g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Creme Fraiche: 200ml
- Parmesan: 50g
- Parsley: 2 tbs chopped
- Butter: 20g
1. Cook the pasta.
2. Fry the sliced mushrooms in the butter over high heat for 6 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the crème fraîche and parmesan and simmer for 2 minutes.
4. Toss with the pasta and parsley.

# Chickpea and spinach curry | Curry de pois chiches aux épinards | 鹰嘴豆菠菜咖喱

- Coconut Milk: 400ml
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Curry Powder: 2 tbs
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Spinach: 200g
- Basmati Rice: 300g

- Celery: 1 stick
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chopped Tomatoes: 800g
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Spaghetti: 400g

# Vegetable fried rice with tofu | Riz sauté aux légumes et tofu | 豆腐蔬菜炒饭

# Quinoa bowl with roasted sweet potato | Bowl de quinoa et patate douce rôtie | 藜麦烤红薯碗
@ Vegan | United States | 35 | File:Vegan Quinoa Bowl (44040185371).jpg | Bowl,Vegetarian
- Quinoa: 250g
- Sweet Potatoes: 2
- Chickpeas: 400g
- Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp
- Avocado: 2
- Rocket: 100g
- Tahini: 3 tbs
- Lemon: 1
1. Roast the sweet potatoes in cubes and the chickpeas with oil and paprika at 220C for 25 minutes.
2. Cook the quinoa in boiling water for 12 minutes and drain.
3. Mix the tahini with lemon juice and water to a pouring sauce.
4. Fill bowls with quinoa, rocket, sweet potato, chickpeas and sliced avocado and drizzle with the sauce.

# Chicken and potato traybake | Poulet et pommes de terre rôtis à la plaque | 烤盘鸡腿土豆
@ Chicken | France | 50 | en:Roast chicken | Roast,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Red Onions: 2
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Rosemary: 3 sprigs
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Lemon: 1
- Lettuce: 1
1. Heat the oven to 210C. Cut the potatoes into wedges and onions into quarters.
2. Toss everything with the oil, rosemary, unpeeled garlic, salt and pepper on a large tray, chicken skin side up.
3. Roast for 45 minutes until the skin is crisp. Squeeze over the lemon and serve with salad.

# Pork chops with apples and mash | Côtes de porc aux pommes et purée | 苹果猪排配土豆泥
@ Pork | France | 35 | en:Pork chop | Family
- Pork Chops: 4
- Apples: 2
- Butter: 40g
- Cider: 150ml
- Floury Potatoes: 900g
- Milk: 150ml
- Thyme: 2 sprigs
1. Boil the potatoes for 20 minutes and mash with half the butter and the milk.
2. Season the chops and fry in the rest of the butter for 5 minutes on each side. Set aside.
3. Fry the apple wedges and thyme in the pan for 4 minutes, add the cider and let it bubble for 2 minutes.
4. Return the chops to warm through and serve with the mash.

- Sunflower Oil: 5 tbs
- Rice: 300g
- Frozen Peas: 300g
- Lemon: 1

# Crispy chicken wraps | Wraps de poulet croustillant | 脆皮鸡肉卷
@ Chicken | United States | 25 | en:Wrap (food) | Quick,Wrap
- Chicken Breasts: 3
- Breadcrumbs: 100g
- Eggs: 1
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Flour Tortilla: 4
- Lettuce: 1/2
- Tomatoes: 2
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbs
- Cheddar Cheese: 80g
1. Cut the chicken into strips, dip in beaten egg then in breadcrumbs mixed with paprika.

# Teriyaki salmon rice bowl | Bol de riz au saumon teriyaki | 照烧三文鱼盖饭

- Soy Sauce: 4 tbs
- Mirin: 3 tbs
- Honey: 1 tbs
- Sushi Rice: 300g
- Broccoli: 1 head
- Sesame Seed: 1 tbs
- Spring Onions: 2

# Pasta with sausage and tomato | Pâtes à la saucisse et tomate | 香肠番茄意面
@ Pork | Italy | 25 | File:Penne with sausage and pumpkin ragù (8462238749).jpg | Quick,Pasta,Family
- Penne Rigate: 400g
- Sausages: 4
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Passata: 500ml
- Creme Fraiche: 3 tbs
- Parmesan: 40g
1. Cook the pasta.
2. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins and fry with the onion for 8 minutes, breaking it up.
3. Add the garlic and passata and simmer for 10 minutes, then stir in the crème fraîche.
4. Toss with the pasta and serve with parmesan.
