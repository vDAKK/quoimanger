# Jollof rice with chicken | Riz jollof au poulet | 西非乔洛夫饭配鸡肉
@ Chicken | Nigeria | 60 | en:Jollof rice | Rice,Family,Spicy
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Rice: 400g
- Red Pepper: 2
- Tomatoes: 4
- Tomato Puree: 3 tbs
- Onions: 2
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Ginger: 1 tbs
- Curry Powder: 2 tsp
- Thyme: 1 tsp
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Chicken Stock: 700ml
- Vegetable Oil: 4 tbs
1. Season the chicken with curry powder, thyme and salt, then brown it in oil and set aside.
2. Blend the peppers, tomatoes, one onion, scotch bonnet, garlic and ginger until smooth.
3. Fry the other onion, sliced, then add the tomato puree and the blended sauce. Cook for 15 minutes until reduced.
4. Add the rinsed rice, stock and bay leaves. Lay the chicken on top, cover tightly and cook on low heat for 30 minutes.
5. Stir gently and serve.

# Nigerian beef stew | Ragoût de bœuf nigérian | 尼日利亚炖牛肉
@ Beef | Nigeria | 90 | File:White rice, plantain and stew (Nigerian dish).jpg | Stew,Spicy
- Beef: 800g
- Tomatoes: 6
- Red Pepper: 3
- Onions: 2
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Ginger: 1 tbs
- Thyme: 1 tsp
- Curry Powder: 1 tsp
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Vegetable Oil: 6 tbs
- Rice: 400g
1. Simmer the beef in water with half an onion, thyme, curry powder and salt for 45 minutes. Keep the stock.
2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, onion, scotch bonnet, garlic and ginger, then boil the purée for 15 minutes to reduce it.
3. Brown the drained beef in oil, add the reduced purée and fry for 10 minutes.
4. Add 300ml of stock and the bay leaves, simmer for 20 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Egusi-style spinach stew | Ragoût d’épinards façon egusi | 西非菠菜炖菜
@ Beef | Nigeria | 60 | en:Efo riro | Stew
- Beef: 500g
- Spinach: 600g
- Red Pepper: 2
- Tomatoes: 3
- Onions: 2
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Dried Shrimp: 2 tbs
- Beef Stock Cubes: 1
- Vegetable Oil: 5 tbs
- Rice: 400g
1. Simmer the beef cut into cubes in seasoned water for 40 minutes.
2. Blend the peppers, tomatoes, one onion and scotch bonnet.
3. Fry the other onion in oil, add the purée and cook for 15 minutes until thick.
4. Add the beef, a little stock, the crushed dried shrimp and the stock cube.
5. Stir in the chopped spinach and cook for 5 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Suya beef skewers | Brochettes de bœuf suya | 苏亚烤牛肉串

- Vegetable Oil: 2 tbs
- Rice: 300g

# Ghanaian groundnut soup | Soupe à l’arachide ghanéenne | 加纳花生汤
@ Chicken | Ghana | 60 | en:Groundnut soup | Soup
- Chicken Legs: 4
- Peanut Butter: 200g
- Tomatoes: 4
- Onions: 2
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Ginger: 1 tbs
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Chicken Stock: 1 litre
- Rice: 300g
1. Brown the chicken with one chopped onion, the ginger and garlic.
2. Blend the tomatoes with the other onion and the scotch bonnet. Add to the pot with the stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Whisk the peanut butter with a ladle of hot broth, then stir it into the pot.
4. Simmer for 25 minutes until the oil rises to the top. Serve with rice.

# Chicken yassa | Poulet yassa | 塞内加尔洋葱柠檬鸡
@ Chicken | Senegal | 75 | en:Yassa (food) | Classic
- Chicken Legs: 6
- Onions: 6
- Lemons: 4
- Dijon Mustard: 2 tbs
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Green Olives: 100g
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Chicken Stock Cube: 1
- Vegetable Oil: 4 tbs
- Rice: 400g
1. Marinate the chicken with the sliced onions, lemon juice, mustard, garlic and salt for at least 1 hour.
2. Grill or pan-fry the chicken pieces until browned.
3. Cook the onions from the marinade in oil for 20 minutes until soft and golden.
4. Add the marinade, the chicken, the stock cube, bay leaves, whole scotch bonnet and olives with a glass of water.
5. Simmer for 25 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Thieboudienne | Thiéboudiène (riz au poisson) | 塞内加尔鱼饭
@ Seafood | Senegal | 90 | en:Thieboudienne | Rice,Fish
- White Fish Fillets: 800g
- Rice: 400g
- Tomato Puree: 4 tbs
- Onions: 2
- Parsley: 1 bunch
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Carrots: 3
- Cabbage: 0.5
- Aubergine: 1
- Cassava: 300g
- Fish Stock: 1.5 litres
- Vegetable Oil: 6 tbs
1. Blend parsley, garlic, scotch bonnet and salt. Stuff the paste into slits cut in the fish.
2. Brown the fish in oil and set aside. Fry the onions in the same oil, then the tomato puree for 5 minutes.
3. Add the stock and the vegetables cut in large pieces. Simmer for 30 minutes, adding the fish for the last 15 minutes.
4. Remove the fish and vegetables. Cook the rice in the broth, covered, for 25 minutes.
5. Serve the rice topped with the fish and vegetables.

# Doro wat | Doro wat (poulet épicé éthiopien) | 埃塞俄比亚辣炖鸡

- Paprika: 2 tbs
- Cayenne Pepper: 2 tsp

- Ground Cardomom: 0.5 tsp
- Ground Cinnamon: 0.5 tsp
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Lemon: 1
- Eggs: 4
- Chicken Stock: 400ml
- Rice: 300g

2. Cook the finely chopped onions in a dry pan for 15 minutes, then add the butter and cook 15 minutes more until very soft.

5. Add hard-boiled eggs scored with a knife for the last 10 minutes. Serve with rice or flatbread.

# Bobotie | Bobotie | 南非咖喱肉饼
@ Beef | South Africa | 75 | en:Bobotie | Bake,Family
- Minced Beef: 800g
- Onions: 2
- Bread: 2 slices
- Milk: 300ml
- Eggs: 3
- Curry Powder: 2 tbs
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Raisins: 60g
- Apricot Jam: 2 tbs
- Lemon: 1
- Bay Leaves: 4
- Rice: 300g
1. Soak the bread in half the milk. Fry the onions until soft, add the curry powder and turmeric.
2. Add the mince and brown it. Stir in the squeezed bread, raisins, jam, lemon juice and salt.
3. Spread in a baking dish. Whisk the eggs with the remaining milk and pour over. Push in the bay leaves.

# Jamaican oxtail stew | Queue de bœuf à la jamaïcaine | 牙买加炖牛尾

- Thyme: 4 sprigs

- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 2 tbs
- Carrots: 2

# Haitian griot | Griot haïtien (porc frit) | 海地炸猪肉
@ Pork | Haiti | 120 | en:Griot (food) | Fried,Classic
- Pork Shoulder: 1kg
- Orange: 2
- Lime: 2
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Thyme: 3 sprigs
- Parsley: 1 bunch
- Scotch Bonnet: 1
- Cabbage: 0.5
- Carrots: 2
- White Vinegar: 200ml
- Vegetable Oil: 500ml
- Rice: 300g
1. Marinate the pork cubes with orange and lime juice, onion, garlic, herbs and scotch bonnet for at least 2 hours.
2. Simmer the pork in its marinade with a little water for 1 hour until tender and the liquid has evaporated.
