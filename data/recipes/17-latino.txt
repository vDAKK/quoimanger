# Cochinita pibil | Cochinita pibil (porc mariné à l’achiote) | 墨西哥胭脂树烤猪肉
@ Pork | Mexico | 200 | en:Cochinita pibil | Tacos,Classic
- Pork Shoulder: 1.2kg
- Achiote Paste: 100g
- Orange Juice: 200ml
- Lime: 3
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Banana Leaves: 2
- Red Onions: 2
- Corn Tortillas: 16
1. Blend the achiote with orange juice, juice of 2 limes, garlic, cumin, oregano and salt. Marinate the pork in it overnight.
2. Wrap the pork and marinade in banana leaves (or foil) in a dish. Cook covered at 160°C for 3 hours.
3. Slice the red onions thinly and steep in the remaining lime juice with salt.
4. Shred the pork in its juices. Serve in warm tortillas with the pickled onions.

# Chicken tinga tostadas | Tostadas au poulet tinga | 墨西哥辣鸡脆饼
@ Chicken | Mexico | 40 | en:Tinga (dish) | Tacos,Spicy,Quick
- Chicken Breasts: 3
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Chipotle Chillies In Adobo: 2
- Onions: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Corn Tortillas: 12
- Refried Beans: 400g
- Lettuce: 1
- Sour Cream: 150ml
- Queso Fresco: 150g
1. Poach the chicken in salted water for 15 minutes and shred it.
2. Blend the tomatoes with chipotle, garlic and oregano. Fry the sliced onions, add the sauce and simmer 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the chicken.
4. Bake the tortillas until crisp. Spread with beans, top with the chicken, lettuce, cream and cheese.

# Pozole rojo | Pozole rojo | 墨西哥红玉米猪肉汤
@ Pork | Mexico | 150 | en:Pozole | Soup,Spicy
- Pork Shoulder: 1kg
- Dried White Corn: 400g
- Ancho Chillies: 4
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Onions: 1
- Dried Oregano: 2 tsp
- Cabbage: 0.25
- Radish: 8
- Lime: 2
- Corn Tortillas: 8
1. Simmer the pork with half an onion, garlic and salt in 3 litres of water for 1 h 30. Add the cooked corn (hominy) for the last 30 minutes.
2. Soak the seeded chillies in hot water, then blend with garlic, onion and a little broth. Strain into the pot.
3. Simmer for 20 minutes more and season.
4. Serve with shredded cabbage, sliced radish, oregano, lime and toasted tortillas.

# Mole poblano chicken | Poulet au mole | 墨西哥巧克力辣酱鸡
@ Chicken | Mexico | 90 | en:Mole poblano | Classic,Spicy
- Chicken Legs: 6
- Ancho Chillies: 4
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Almonds: 50g
- Raisins: 40g
- Sesame Seed: 3 tbs
- Corn Tortillas: 2
- Cocoa: 2 tbs
- Ground Cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Ground Clove: 0.25 tsp
- Chicken Stock: 600ml
- Rice: 400g
1. Poach the chicken in the stock for 35 minutes. Keep the stock.
2. Toast the chillies, soak them, then fry the onion, garlic, almonds, raisins, half the sesame and torn tortillas.
3. Blend everything with the tomatoes, cocoa, spices and 400ml stock until smooth.
4. Fry the sauce in a little oil for 15 minutes. Add the chicken, simmer 10 minutes and sprinkle with sesame. Serve with rice.

# Chiles rellenos | Chiles rellenos (poivrons farcis au fromage) | 墨西哥奶酪酿辣椒
@ Vegetarian | Mexico | 60 | en:Chile relleno | Vegetarian,Fried,Cheese
- Green Pepper: 4
- Monterey Jack Cheese: 250g
- Eggs: 4
- Plain Flour: 50g
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Jalapeno: 1
- Vegetable Oil: 300ml
- Rice: 300g
1. Roast the peppers under the grill until blackened, steam them in a bag, then peel and slit open. Remove the seeds.
2. Stuff with cheese. Simmer the tomatoes with the onion, garlic and jalapeno into a sauce.
3. Whip the egg whites stiff, fold in the yolks. Dust the peppers with flour, dip in the batter and fry until golden.
4. Serve on the tomato sauce with rice.

# Enchiladas verdes | Enchiladas vertes au poulet | 墨西哥绿酱卷饼
@ Chicken | Mexico | 50 | en:Enchilada | Bake,Family

- Green Salsa: 500ml
- Corn Tortillas: 12
- Sour Cream: 200ml
- Monterey Jack Cheese: 200g
- Onions: 1
- Coriander: 1 bunch
1. Poach and shred the chicken. Mix with a little salsa and chopped onion.
2. Warm the tortillas, fill with chicken and roll. Place in a dish.

# Pupusas with curtido | Pupusas et curtido | 萨尔瓦多玉米饼
@ Vegetarian | El Salvador | 60 | en:Pupusa | Vegetarian,Cheese
- Yellow Masarepa: 400g
- Refried Beans: 200g
- Mozzarella: 250g
- Cabbage: 0.5
- Carrots: 1
- Onions: 0.5
- White Vinegar: 150ml
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Tomato Sauce: 200ml

2. Knead the corn flour with 500ml warm water and salt.

# Guatemalan pepian | Pepián de poulet | 危地马拉炖鸡
@ Chicken | Guatemala | 75 | en:Pepián | Stew
- Chicken Legs: 6
- Tomatoes: 5
- Red Pepper: 1
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Sesame Seed: 3 tbs
- Pumpkin: 50g
- Dried Chillies: 2
- Ground Cinnamon: 0.5 tsp
- Potatoes: 3
- Green Beans: 200g
- Rice: 300g
1. Simmer the chicken in 1 litre of salted water for 25 minutes.
2. Toast the tomatoes, pepper, onion, garlic, sesame, pumpkin seeds and chillies in a dry pan until charred.
3. Blend with the cinnamon and some broth.
4. Add the sauce, potatoes and beans to the chicken and simmer 25 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Puerto Rican pernil with rice | Pernil portoricain et riz | 波多黎各烤猪肩配米饭
@ Pork | Puerto Rico | 240 | en:Pernil | Roast,Family
- Pork Shoulder: 2kg
- Garlic: 10 cloves
- Dried Oregano: 1 tbs
- Olive Oil: 3 tbs
- White Vinegar: 2 tbs
- Sazon: 2 tsp
- Rice: 400g
- Pinto Beans: 400g
- Green Pepper: 1
- Onions: 1
1. Crush the garlic with oregano, oil, vinegar, sazon and plenty of salt. Rub into slits all over the pork. Marinate overnight.
2. Roast covered at 160°C for 3 hours, then uncovered at 220°C for 30 minutes until the skin crackles.
3. Fry the onion and pepper, add the rice, beans and 700ml water and cook covered for 20 minutes.
4. Slice the pork and serve with the rice.

# Mofongo with garlic shrimp | Mofongo et crevettes à l’ail | 波多黎各捣芭蕉配蒜香虾
@ Seafood | Puerto Rico | 40 | en:Mofongo | Fried
- Plantain: 4
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Bacon: 4 slices
- King Prawns: 400g
- Chopped Tomatoes: 200g
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Chicken Stock: 150ml
- Coriander: 1 handful
- Vegetable Oil: 500ml
1. Fry chunks of green plantain in oil for 8 minutes. Fry the bacon until crisp.
2. Mash the plantain with half the garlic, the bacon, a little oil and stock.
3. Sauté the prawns in olive oil with the remaining garlic, add the tomatoes and cook 3 minutes.
4. Shape the mofongo into domes and serve with the prawns and coriander.

# Ajiaco | Ajiaco (soupe colombienne poulet-pommes de terre) | 哥伦比亚鸡肉土豆汤
@ Chicken | Colombia | 75 | en:Ajiaco | Soup
- Chicken Breasts: 3
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Corn On The Cob: 2
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Chicken Stock: 2 litres
- Double Cream: 150ml
- Capers: 2 tbs
- Avocado: 2
1. Simmer the chicken with the onion, garlic and coriander stems in the stock for 25 minutes. Shred.
2. Add the potatoes in slices (some fine, some thicker) and the corn in rounds. Cook 40 minutes until the fine potatoes melt into the soup.
3. Return the chicken.
4. Serve with cream, capers, avocado and coriander.

# Argentine steak with chimichurri | Steak argentin et chimichurri | 阿根廷牛排配青酱

# Peruvian aji de gallina | Ají de gallina | 秘鲁辣奶油鸡
@ Chicken | Peru | 50 | en:Ají de gallina | Spicy,Family
- Chicken Breasts: 3
- Bread: 4 slices
- Evaporated Milk: 300ml
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Yellow Pepper: 2
- Green Chilli: 2
- Walnuts: 50g
- Parmesan: 40g
- Ground Turmeric: 0.5 tsp
- Potatoes: 4
- Black Olives: 8
- Eggs: 2
- Rice: 300g
1. Poach the chicken for 20 minutes and shred it. Keep some broth. Soak the bread in the milk.
2. Blend the peppers and chillies. Fry the onion and garlic, add the pepper paste and turmeric.

# Peruvian arroz chaufa | Arroz chaufa (riz sauté péruvien) | 秘鲁中式炒饭

- Chicken Breasts: 2
- Eggs: 3
- Red Pepper: 1
- Spring Onions: 4
- Ginger: 1 tbs
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Soy Sauce: 4 tbs
- Sesame Seed Oil: 1 tbs
- Vegetable Oil: 3 tbs

2. Scramble the eggs in a hot wok and set aside.
3. Stir-fry the diced chicken with ginger and garlic, then the pepper.

# Moqueca | Moqueca (ragoût de poisson au lait de coco) | 巴西椰奶炖鱼
@ Seafood | Brazil | 45 | en:Moqueca | Fish,Stew
- White Fish Fillets: 700g
- Prawns: 200g
- Coconut Milk: 400ml
- Onions: 1
- Red Pepper: 1
- Yellow Pepper: 1
- Tomatoes: 3
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Lime: 2
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Rice: 300g
1. Marinate the fish with lime juice, garlic and salt for 20 minutes.
2. Layer the onion, peppers and tomatoes in rings in a wide pan, then the fish.
3. Pour over the coconut milk mixed with paprika. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, adding the prawns for the last 5.
4. Sprinkle with coriander and serve with rice.
