# Baked ziti | Penne au four à l’italo-américaine | 美式焗通心粉
@ Pasta | United States | 50 | en:Baked ziti | Pasta,Bake,Family
- Penne Rigate: 400g
- Italian Fennel Sausages: 400g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Passata: 700ml
- Ricotta: 250g
- Mozzarella: 250g
- Parmesan: 50g
- Basil: 1 handful
1. Brown the sausage meat with the onion and garlic. Add the passata and simmer 15 minutes.
2. Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet time.
3. Mix the pasta with the sauce and layer in a dish with spoonfuls of ricotta and torn mozzarella.
4. Top with parmesan and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Add basil.

# Spaghetti and meatballs | Spaghetti aux boulettes | 意式肉丸意面
@ Pasta | United States | 50 | en:Spaghetti and meatballs | Pasta,Family
- Spaghetti: 400g
- Minced Beef: 400g
- Minced Pork: 200g
- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Milk: 80ml
- Eggs: 1
- Parmesan: 60g
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Passata: 700ml
- Onions: 1
- Basil: 1 handful
1. Mix the meats with breadcrumbs soaked in milk, egg, half the parmesan, garlic, parsley and salt. Roll into balls.
2. Brown the meatballs. Soften the onion, add the passata and simmer the meatballs in it for 20 minutes.
3. Cook the spaghetti.
4. Serve with the sauce, meatballs, basil and parmesan.

# Pasta alla genovese | Pâtes à la genovese (ragù d’oignons) | 那不勒斯洋葱牛肉酱面
@ Pasta | Italy | 210 | en:Genovese sauce | Pasta,Classic
- Rigatoni: 400g
- Beef: 600g
- Onions: 1.5kg
- Carrots: 1
- Celery: 1 stick
- Dry White Wine: 150ml
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Parmesan: 60g
1. Brown the beef in large pieces in oil with the chopped carrot and celery.
2. Add the thinly sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cover and cook very gently for 3 hours, stirring now and then, adding the wine halfway.
3. Shred the beef into the golden onion sauce.
4. Toss with the cooked pasta and parmesan.

# Pasta with pumpkin and sage | Pâtes à la courge et sauge | 南瓜鼠尾草意面
@ Pasta | Italy | 35 | File:Pasta with pumpkin, aubergine and chili pepper (30506843925).jpg | Pasta,Vegetarian,Quick
- Farfalle: 400g
- Butternut Squash: 1
- Sage: 1 bunch
- Butter: 60g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Parmesan: 60g
- Walnuts: 50g
1. Cut the squash into small cubes and roast with oil at 220°C for 25 minutes.
2. Fry the sage leaves in the butter until crisp, add the garlic.
3. Cook the pasta and toss with the squash, sage butter and a little pasta water.
4. Top with parmesan and toasted walnuts.

# Pasta with broccoli and anchovies | Pâtes au brocoli et anchois | 西兰花凤尾鱼意面

- Anchovy Fillet: 8
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Chilli Flakes: 0.5 tsp
- Olive Oil: 6 tbs
- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Pecorino: 50g
1. Cook the broccoli florets with the pasta in the same water.
2. Melt the anchovies in oil with the garlic and chilli.

# Mushroom bourguignon | Bourguignon de champignons | 红酒炖蘑菇
@ Vegan | France | 60 | File:Mushroom Bourguignon with roasted parsnip and creamed potatoes (GOC Kings Langley Xmas 2012 084) (8290538877).jpg | Vegetarian,Stew
- Chestnut Mushroom: 600g
- Shallots: 8
- Carrots: 3
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Red Wine: 400ml
- Vegetable Stock: 300ml
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Thyme: 3 sprigs
- Bay Leaf: 1
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Potatoes: 1kg
1. Brown the mushrooms in batches in hot oil. Set aside.
2. Fry the shallots and carrots, add the garlic, tomato puree and flour.
3. Add the wine, stock and herbs and simmer 25 minutes. Return the mushrooms for 10 minutes.
4. Serve with mashed potatoes made with olive oil.

# Tofu katsu curry | Curry katsu au tofu | 日式炸豆腐咖喱
@ Vegan | Japan | 45 | en:Katsu curry | Curry,Vegetarian
- Tofu: 600g
- Plain Flour: 60g
- Soya Milk: 150ml
- Panko: 120g
- Onions: 1
- Carrots: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Curry Powder: 2 tbs
- Vegetable Stock: 500ml
- Soy Sauce: 1 tbs
- Maple Syrup: 1 tbs
- Sushi Rice: 300g
- Vegetable Oil: 200ml
1. Fry the onion and carrots, add the garlic and curry powder, then 1 tbs flour, the stock, soy sauce and syrup. Simmer 15 minutes and blend.
2. Press the tofu and cut into thick slices.
3. Coat in flour, soya milk and panko and fry until golden.
4. Serve on rice with the sauce.

- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Corn Flour: 2 tbs
- Broccoli: 1
- Edamame Beans: 200g
- Carrots: 2
- Sesame Seed: 2 tbs
- Sushi Rice: 300g
- Vegetable Oil: 2 tbs

2. Cut the tofu into cubes, toss in corn flour and fry until crisp.

# Rice and lentil stuffed peppers | Poivrons farcis riz et lentilles | 米饭扁豆酿甜椒
@ Vegan | Greece | 70 | en:Stuffed peppers | Vegetarian,Bake
- Red Pepper: 4
- Rice: 150g
- Lentils: 150g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Mint: 1 handful
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Potatoes: 4
1. Cook the lentils for 15 minutes.
2. Fry the onion and garlic, add the rice, lentils, half the tomatoes, herbs and salt.
3. Fill the halved peppers and place in a dish with potato wedges and the remaining tomatoes.
4. Drizzle with oil, add a glass of water and bake covered at 190°C for 40 minutes, then uncovered for 15.

# Vegan mushroom stroganoff | Stroganov végétal aux champignons | 纯素蘑菇斯特罗加诺夫
@ Vegan | Russia | 30 | File:Beef Stroganoff z boczniaka (Poznan).jpg | Vegetarian,Quick
- Mushrooms: 700g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Cashew Nuts: 100g
- Dijon Mustard: 1 tbs
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Vegetable Stock: 200ml
- Olive Oil: 3 tbs
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Rice: 300g
1. Soak the cashews in boiling water for 15 minutes and blend with 200ml water until smooth.
2. Fry the mushrooms in batches until browned, then the onions and garlic.
3. Add the paprika, mustard, stock and cashew cream and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Serve with rice and parsley.
