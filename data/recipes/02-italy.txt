# Mushroom risotto | Risotto aux champignons | 蘑菇烩饭
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 40 | File:Mushroom risotto (3990739885).jpg | Rice,Vegetarian
- Arborio Rice: 320g
- Mushrooms: 400g
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Dry White Wine: 100ml
- Vegetable Stock: 1.2 litres
- Butter: 40g
- Parmesan: 60g
- Parsley: 2 tbs chopped
1. Keep the stock hot in a pan. Fry the sliced mushrooms in half the butter for 6 minutes and set aside.
2. Soften the chopped onion and garlic in the rest of the butter for 4 minutes. Add the rice and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate, then add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 minutes until the rice is creamy and just tender.
4. Stir in the mushrooms, parmesan and parsley. Rest for 2 minutes before serving.

# Risotto alla milanese | Risotto à la milanaise | 米兰藏红花烩饭
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 35 | File:Risotto alla Milanese.JPG | Rice
- Arborio Rice: 320g
- Saffron: 1 pinch
- Onion: 1
- Dry White Wine: 100ml
- Chicken Stock: 1.2 litres
- Butter: 50g
- Parmesan: 70g
1. Soak the saffron in a ladle of hot stock.
2. Soften the chopped onion in half the butter for 4 minutes, add the rice and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the wine, let it evaporate, then add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 minutes. Add the saffron halfway.
4. Off the heat, beat in the rest of the butter and the parmesan.

# Pasta with basil pesto | Pâtes au pesto | 罗勒青酱意面
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 20 | en:Pesto | Quick,Pasta,Vegetarian
- Farfalle: 400g
- Basil Leaves: 60g
- Pine Nuts: 40g
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Parmesan: 50g
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil: 120ml
- Cherry Tomatoes: 200g
1. Cook the pasta following the packet instructions.
2. Blend the basil, pine nuts, garlic and parmesan, then blend in the olive oil and a pinch of salt.
3. Drain the pasta, keeping a cup of the cooking water. Toss with the pesto, loosening with a little water.
4. Serve topped with halved cherry tomatoes.

# Pasta e fagioli | Pasta e fagioli (pâtes aux haricots) | 意式豆子意面汤
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 40 | en:Pasta e fagioli | Soup,Vegetarian
- Macaroni: 200g
- Borlotti Beans: 800g
- Onion: 1
- Carrots: 1
- Celery: 1 stick
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Vegetable Stock: 1 litre
- Rosemary: 1 sprig
- Parmesan: 40g
1. Soften the chopped onion, carrot, celery and garlic in olive oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes, half the beans, the rosemary and the stock and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Mash the remaining beans and add them with the pasta. Cook for 10 minutes until the pasta is tender.
4. Serve with grated parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.

# Pasta alla Norma | Pâtes alla Norma | 西西里茄子意面
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 40 | en:Pasta alla Norma | Pasta,Vegetarian

- Aubergine: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chopped Tomatoes: 800g
- Basil: 1 handful
- Ricotta: 100g
- Olive Oil: 6 tbs
- Chilli Flakes: 1 pinch
1. Cut the aubergines into cubes and fry in the oil for 12 minutes until golden. Drain.

# Spaghetti aglio e olio | Spaghetti aglio e olio | 蒜香橄榄油意面
@ Vegan | Italy | 15 | en:Spaghetti aglio e olio | Quick,Pasta
- Spaghetti: 400g
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil: 100ml
- Chilli Flakes: 1 tsp
- Parsley: 4 tbs chopped
- Lemon: 1/2
1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water.
2. Meanwhile warm the oil with the thinly sliced garlic and chilli over low heat for 4 minutes until the garlic is pale golden.
3. Drain the pasta, keeping some water, and toss in the oil with the parsley, a squeeze of lemon and a splash of the cooking water.

# Cacio e pepe | Cacio e pepe | 黑胡椒奶酪意面
@ Vegetarian | Italy | 15 | en:Cacio e pepe | Quick,Pasta
- Spaghetti: 400g
- Pecorino: 120g
- Parmesan: 40g
- Black Pepper: 2 tsp
- Butter: 20g
1. Cook the spaghetti in lightly salted water.
2. Toast the coarsely ground pepper in a large pan for 1 minute, then add the butter and a ladle of pasta water.
3. Mix the finely grated cheeses with a little warm pasta water to make a paste.
4. Add the drained pasta to the pan, then the cheese paste, tossing quickly off the heat and adding water until creamy.

# Gnocchi alla sorrentina | Gnocchis à la sorrentine | 索伦托焗土豆团子

- Gnocchi: 800g
- Passata: 500ml
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Mozzarella: 250g
- Basil: 1 handful
- Parmesan: 40g
1. Simmer the passata with the garlic and a pinch of salt for 10 minutes.
2. Boil the gnocchi until they float, about 2 minutes. Drain.

# Ham and mushroom pizza | Pizza reine | 火腿蘑菇披萨
@ Pork | Italy | 40 | en:Pizza | Pizza,Family
- Bread Flour: 400g
- Fast Action Yeast: 7g
- Water: 250ml
- Olive Oil: 2 tbs
- Passata: 200ml
- Mozzarella: 250g
- Ham: 150g
- Mushrooms: 150g
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
1. Mix the flour, yeast, a pinch of salt, the water and oil. Knead for 8 minutes and leave to rise for 1 hour.
2. Heat the oven to 250C. Divide the dough in two and stretch into thin rounds.
3. Spread with passata, then add the mozzarella, ham strips and sliced mushrooms. Sprinkle with oregano.
4. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until crisp.

- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Milk: 50ml
- Egg: 1
- Parmesan: 40g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Passata: 700ml
- Basil: 1 handful
- Spaghetti: 400g

- Mozzarella: 250g
- Parmesan: 80g
- Basil: 1 handful
- Olive Oil: 6 tbs
- Crusty Bread: 1

# Calzone with ham and ricotta | Calzone jambon ricotta | 火腿乳清干酪披萨饺
@ Pork | Italy | 45 | en:Calzone | Pizza
- Bread Flour: 400g
- Fast Action Yeast: 7g
- Water: 250ml
- Ricotta: 250g
- Mozzarella: 200g
- Ham: 150g
- Passata: 150ml
- Basil: 1 handful
1. Mix the flour, yeast, a pinch of salt and the water, knead for 8 minutes and leave to rise for 1 hour.
2. Heat the oven to 240C. Divide the dough in four and roll out into rounds.
3. Spread passata on one half of each, add ricotta, mozzarella, ham and basil. Fold over and seal the edges.
4. Bake for 15 minutes until puffed and golden.
