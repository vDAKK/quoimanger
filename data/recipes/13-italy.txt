# Penne alla vodka | Penne alla vodka | 伏特加番茄奶油通心粉
@ Pasta | Italy | 25 | en:Penne alla vodka | Quick
- Penne Rigate: 400g
- Passata: 400ml
- Double Cream: 150ml
- Vodka: 50ml
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Chilli Flakes: 1/2 tsp
- Butter: 30g
- Parmesan: 60g
1. Cook the pasta.
2. Soften the onion and garlic in the butter, add the chilli and vodka and reduce for 1 minute.
3. Add the passata and simmer for 10 minutes, then stir in the cream.
4. Toss the pasta in the sauce with the parmesan and a little cooking water.

# Arancini | Arancini (boules de risotto frites) | 西西里炸饭团

- Frozen Peas: 80g
- Eggs: 2
- Plain Flour: 60g
- Breadcrumbs: 150g
- Vegetable Oil: 800ml
- Passata: 300ml

# Pici all'aglione | Pici à l'aglione (sauce tomate à l'ail) | 托斯卡纳大蒜番茄粗面
@ Vegan | Italy | 40 | en:Pici
- Spaghetti: 400g
- Garlic: 8 cloves
- Plum Tomatoes: 800g
- Chilli Flakes: 1/2 tsp
- Olive Oil: 6 tbs
- Pecorino: 50g
1. Use pici or thick spaghetti. Slice the garlic and cook it very gently in the oil for 10 minutes without colouring.
2. Add the chilli and tomatoes and simmer for 25 minutes, mashing.
3. Cook the pasta and toss in the sauce.
4. Serve with pecorino.

# Sausage and radicchio risotto | Risotto à la saucisse | 香肠烩饭
@ Pork | Italy | 40 | en:Risotto
- Arborio Rice: 320g
- Italian Fennel Sausages: 4
- Onion: 1
- Red Wine: 120ml
- Chicken Stock: 1.2l
- Butter: 40g
- Parmesan: 60g
- Red Cabbage: 150g
1. Fry the crumbled sausage and the onion.
2. Add the rice, toast for 1 minute, then the wine.
3. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18 minutes, adding the shredded cabbage halfway.
4. Finish with butter and parmesan.
