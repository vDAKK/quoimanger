# Garlic chicken | Poulet à l'ail (pollo al ajillo) | 西班牙蒜香鸡
@ Chicken | Spain | 40 | File:Pollo al ajillo.jpg | Classic
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Garlic: 12 cloves
- Dry White Wine: 150ml
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Parsley: 3 tbs chopped
- Potatoes: 700g
1. Cut the potatoes into cubes and roast them with a little oil at 210C for 30 minutes.
2. Brown the seasoned chicken in the oil for 10 minutes. Add the halved garlic cloves and bay leaves and cook for 3 minutes.
3. Pour in the wine, cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with the potatoes.

# Fabada asturiana | Fabada asturienne | 阿斯图里亚斯炖豆
@ Pork | Spain | 70 | en:Fabada asturiana | Stew
- Butter Beans: 800g
- Chorizo: 200g
- Morcilla: 150g
- Bacon: 150g
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp
- Saffron: 1 pinch
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Put the bacon, whole chorizo and onion in a pot with 1 litre of water and simmer for 30 minutes.
2. Add the beans, garlic, paprika and saffron and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Add the morcilla for the last 10 minutes. Slice the meats and serve with the beans and bread.

# Spanish lentil stew with chorizo | Lentilles à l'espagnole au chorizo | 西班牙香肠炖扁豆
@ Pork | Spain | 45 | en:Lentil soup | Stew
- Brown Lentils: 300g
- Chorizo: 150g
- Onion: 1
- Carrots: 2
- Potatoes: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp
- Bay Leaf: 1
- Chopped Tomatoes: 200g
1. Fry the sliced chorizo for 3 minutes, add the onion, carrots and garlic and cook for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the paprika, then add the lentils, diced potatoes, tomatoes, bay leaf and 1.2 litres of water.
3. Simmer for 30 minutes until the lentils are tender. Season and serve.

# Riojan potatoes with chorizo | Pommes de terre à la riojana | 里奥哈土豆炖香肠
@ Pork | Spain | 45 | File:Patatas a la riojana.jpg | Stew
- Potatoes: 1kg

- Onion: 1
- Green Pepper: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Smoked Paprika: 2 tsp
- Bay Leaf: 1
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Fry the sliced chorizo for 3 minutes, then the onion, pepper and garlic for 5 minutes.

# Spanakopita | Spanakopita (tourte grecque aux épinards) | 希腊菠菜派
@ Vegetarian | Greece | 60 | en:Spanakopita | Pie,Vegetarian
- Spinach: 800g
- Feta: 250g
- Eggs: 2
- Spring Onions: 6
- Dill: 1 bunch
- Filo Pastry: 250g
- Olive Oil: 6 tbs
- Lettuce: 1
1. Wilt the spinach, squeeze it very dry and chop.
2. Mix with the crumbled feta, eggs, sliced spring onions and dill.
3. Heat the oven to 190C. Layer half the filo sheets in an oiled dish, brushing each with oil, add the filling and cover with the remaining sheets, oiling each.
4. Score the top and bake for 40 minutes until golden. Serve with salad.

# Pastitsio | Pastitsio (gratin grec de pâtes) | 希腊焗通心粉
@ Beef | Greece | 75 | en:Pastitsio | Bake,Family
- Macaroni: 400g
- Minced Beef: 500g
- Onion: 1
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Ground Cinnamon: 1/2 tsp
- Milk: 700ml
- Butter: 60g
- Plain Flour: 60g
- Eggs: 2
- Parmesan: 80g
1. Fry the onion and beef for 8 minutes, add the tomatoes and cinnamon and simmer for 20 minutes.
2. Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than the packet says.
3. Make a béchamel with the butter, flour and milk, simmer for 5 minutes, then off the heat beat in the eggs and half the cheese.
4. Heat the oven to 180C. Layer half the pasta, the meat, the rest of the pasta and the béchamel, sprinkle with the cheese and bake for 40 minutes.

# Pork gyros with tzatziki | Gyros de porc et tzatziki | 希腊猪肉卷饼
@ Pork | Greece | 35 | en:Gyros | Wrap
- Pork Shoulder Steaks: 600g
- Dried Oregano: 2 tsp
- Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Lemon: 1
- Greek Yogurt: 250g
- Cucumber: 1
- Pita Bread: 4
- Tomatoes: 2
- Red Onions: 1
- Fries: 400g
1. Slice the pork thinly and marinate with the oregano, paprika, 2 garlic cloves, lemon juice and olive oil for 15 minutes.
2. Grate the cucumber, squeeze it and mix with the yogurt and remaining garlic.
3. Cook the fries in the oven following the packet. Fry the pork in a hot pan for 6 minutes until charred.
4. Fill the warm pitas with pork, tzatziki, tomato, onion and a few fries.

# Lamb kleftiko | Kleftiko (agneau confit grec) | 希腊烤羊肉
@ Lamb | Greece | 180 | en:Kleftiko | Roast
- Lamb Shoulder: 1.2kg
- Potatoes: 800g
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Lemon: 2
- Dried Oregano: 2 tsp
- Tomatoes: 2
- Red Onions: 2
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Feta: 100g
1. Rub the lamb with garlic, lemon juice, oregano, oil, salt and pepper.
2. Put it in a large dish with the potatoes, onions and tomatoes in chunks, cover tightly with foil.
3. Bake at 160C for 2 hours 30 minutes until the meat falls apart.
4. Crumble the feta on top and serve.

# Chicken lemon rice soup | Soupe grecque poulet citron (avgolemono) | 希腊柠檬鸡汤
@ Chicken | Greece | 40 | en:Avgolemono | Soup
- Chicken Breasts: 2
- Chicken Stock: 1.5 litres
- Rice: 100g
- Eggs: 3
- Lemon: 2
- Dill: 2 tbs chopped
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Poach the chicken in the stock for 15 minutes, lift out and shred.
2. Cook the rice in the stock for 15 minutes.
3. Whisk the eggs with the lemon juice, then whisk in two ladles of hot stock.
4. Off the heat, stir the egg mixture into the soup, add the chicken and dill and warm gently without boiling.

# Turkish kofte with bulgur pilaf | Köfte et pilaf de boulgour | 土耳其肉丸配碎麦饭
@ Lamb | Turkey | 40 | File:Turkish kofte, bulgur and fries.jpg | Grill
- Lamb Mince: 500g
- Onion: 1
- Parsley: 3 tbs chopped
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Bulgur Wheat: 250g
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Vegetable Stock: 500ml
- Greek Yogurt: 150g
- Tomatoes: 2
1. Mix the lamb with the grated onion, parsley, spices and salt. Shape into 16 small oval kofte.
2. Fry the bulgur with the tomato purée in a little oil for 2 minutes, add the stock, cover and cook for 15 minutes.
3. Grill or pan-fry the kofte for 8 minutes, turning.
4. Serve with the pilaf, yogurt and sliced tomatoes.

# Couscous royal | Couscous royal | 皇家古斯米
