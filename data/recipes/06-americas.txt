# Chili con carne | Chili con carne | 墨西哥辣肉酱
@ Beef | Mexico | 60 | en:Chili con carne | Stew,Spicy,Family
- Minced Beef: 500g
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Red Pepper: 1
- Chilli Powder: 2 tsp
- Ground Cumin: 2 tsp
- Chopped Tomatoes: 800g
- Kidney Beans: 400g
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Rice: 300g
- Sour Cream: 100ml
1. Fry the onion, garlic and diced pepper for 5 minutes. Add the beef and brown for 6 minutes.
2. Stir in the spices and tomato purée, then the tomatoes and 150ml water. Simmer for 30 minutes.
3. Add the beans and cook for 10 minutes more.
4. Serve with rice and sour cream.

# Pork carnitas tacos | Tacos de carnitas | 墨西哥慢炖猪肉塔可
@ Pork | Mexico | 180 | en:Carnitas | Tacos
- Pork Shoulder: 1.2kg
- Orange: 1
- Lime: 2
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Ground Cumin: 2 tsp
- Dried Oregano: 1 tsp
- Corn Tortillas: 12
- Red Onions: 1
- Coriander: 1 handful
- Salsa: 200g
1. Cut the pork into large chunks and put in a casserole with the orange juice, juice of 1 lime, garlic, spices, salt and water to half cover.
2. Cover and cook at 160C for 2 hours 30 minutes until very tender.
3. Shred the meat and fry it in a hot pan with some of its fat for 5 minutes until crisp.
4. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, coriander, salsa and lime.

# Chicken quesadillas | Quesadillas au poulet | 鸡肉芝士薄饼
@ Chicken | Mexico | 25 | File:Chicken quesadilla - Massachusetts.jpg | Quick,Family

- Chicken Breasts: 2
- Shredded Mexican Cheese: 250g
- Sweetcorn: 150g
- Black Beans: 200g
- Fajita Seasoning: 2 tsp
- Sour Cream: 100ml
- Salsa: 150g
1. Slice the chicken, toss with the seasoning and fry for 7 minutes.
2. Lay 4 tortillas flat and cover with cheese, chicken, corn and beans. Top with the other tortillas.

4. Cut into wedges and serve with sour cream and salsa.

# Fish and chips | Fish and chips | 炸鱼薯条

- Beer: 300ml
- Baking Powder: 1 tsp
- Floury Potatoes: 1kg
- Sunflower Oil: For frying
- Frozen Peas: 300g
- Lemon: 1
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbs

# Smoked haddock kedgeree-style rice | Riz au haddock fumé et œufs | 熏黑线鳕鸡蛋饭

- Butter: 30g
- Frozen Peas: 150g
- Parsley: 3 tbs chopped
- Lemon: 1
