# Yangzhou fried rice | Riz cantonais | 扬州炒饭
@ Pork | China | 25 | en:Yangzhou fried rice | Quick,Rice,Family
- Rice: 300g
- Eggs: 3
- Ham: 150g
- Prawns: 150g
- Frozen Peas: 100g
- Carrots: 1
- Spring Onions: 3
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Vegetable Oil: 3 tbs
1. Cook the rice the day before if possible, or cook it, spread it out and let it cool completely.
2. Beat the eggs, scramble them in a hot wok with a little oil and set aside.
3. Stir-fry the diced carrot, peas, diced ham and prawns for 3 minutes.
4. Add the rice and stir-fry over high heat for 4 minutes, breaking up the lumps.
5. Return the eggs, add the soy sauce and sliced spring onions and toss for 1 minute.

# Hainanese chicken rice | Riz au poulet hainanais | 海南鸡饭
@ Chicken | China | 70 | en:Hainanese chicken rice | Rice,Family
- Chicken Legs: 4
- Jasmine Rice: 320g
- Ginger: 60g
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Spring Onions: 4
- Chicken Stock: 600ml
- Cucumber: 1
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Sesame Seed Oil: 1 tbs
- Red Chilli: 2
- Lime: 1
1. Bring a large pan of water to the boil with half the ginger sliced and 2 spring onions. Add the chicken, simmer gently for 30 minutes, then plunge into iced water.
2. Fry 4 chopped garlic cloves and a little grated ginger in a spoon of oil, add the rinsed rice and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Cook the rice in 600ml of the chicken poaching broth or stock until tender.
4. Blend the remaining ginger and garlic with the chillies, lime juice and a pinch of salt to make a sauce.
5. Rub the chicken with sesame oil, slice it and serve on the rice with sliced cucumber, soy sauce and the chilli sauce.

# Yuxiang shredded pork | Porc émincé à la sichuanaise (yuxiang rousi) | 鱼香肉丝
@ Pork | China | 30 | en:Yuxiang shredded pork | Quick
- Pork Tenderloin: 400g
- Wood Ear Mushrooms: 20g
- Carrots: 1
- Green Pepper: 1
- Doubanjiang: 2 tbs
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Ginger: 1 tbs grated
- Spring Onions: 3
- Chinkiang Vinegar: 2 tbs
- Sugar: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Cornstarch: 2 tsp
- Rice: 300g
1. Soak the wood ear mushrooms in warm water for 20 minutes, then slice them. Cook the rice.
2. Cut the pork into thin strips and mix with 1 tbs soy sauce and 1 tsp cornstarch.
3. Mix the vinegar, sugar, remaining soy sauce and cornstarch with 4 tbs water.
4. Stir-fry the pork in a hot wok until it changes colour, then set aside.
5. Fry the doubanjiang, garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, add the carrot, pepper and mushrooms in strips and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
6. Return the pork, pour in the sauce and toss until glossy. Sprinkle with spring onions and serve with rice.

# Moo shu pork | Porc moo shu | 木须肉
@ Pork | China | 30 | en:Moo shu pork | Quick
- Pork Tenderloin: 350g
- Eggs: 4
- Wood Ear Mushrooms: 15g
- Cabbage: 200g
- Carrots: 1
- Spring Onions: 4
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Hoisin Sauce: 3 tbs
- Shaoxing Wine: 1 tbs
- Flour Tortilla: 8
1. Soak the wood ear mushrooms for 20 minutes and slice them.
2. Cut the pork into strips and marinate with 1 tbs soy sauce and the Shaoxing wine.
3. Scramble the eggs in a hot wok and set aside.
4. Stir-fry the pork for 2 minutes, add the shredded cabbage, carrot and mushrooms and cook for 3 minutes.
5. Return the eggs with the remaining soy sauce and spring onions.
6. Warm the tortillas, spread with hoisin sauce and roll up with the filling.

# Lion's head meatballs | Têtes de lion (boulettes de porc au chou) | 红烧狮子头
@ Pork | China | 60 | en:Lion's head (food) | Family
- Minced Pork: 600g
- Water Chestnut: 100g
- Eggs: 1
- Ginger: 1 tbs grated
- Spring Onions: 3
- Cornstarch: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 4 tbs
- Shaoxing Wine: 2 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Napa Cabbage: 500g
- Chicken Stock: 500ml
- Rice: 300g
1. Mix the pork with the chopped water chestnuts, egg, ginger, spring onions, 1 tbs cornstarch, 2 tbs soy sauce and the wine. Beat well for 2 minutes.
2. Shape 8 large balls and brown them in oil on all sides.
3. Line a casserole with the cabbage, place the meatballs on top and add the stock, remaining soy sauce and sugar.

# Dongpo pork | Porc Dongpo | 东坡肉
@ Pork | China | 150 | en:Dongpo pork | Family

- Shaoxing Wine: 200ml
- Soy Sauce: 4 tbs
- Dark Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 40g
- Ginger: 40g
- Spring Onions: 6
- Pak Choi: 4
- Rice: 300g
1. Blanch the pork in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and cut into large squares.
2. Line a heavy pan with the spring onions and sliced ginger, add the pork skin side down.
3. Add the wine, soy sauces, sugar and enough water to come halfway up the pork.
4. Cover and simmer very gently for 2 hours, turning the pieces once.
5. Reduce the sauce until syrupy. Serve with rice and blanched pak choi.

- Ginger: 4 slices
- Spring Onions: 2
- Star Anise: 1
- Rice: 300g
- Pak Choi: 4

2. Brown the wings in a little oil with the ginger for 5 minutes.

# Scallion pancakes | Galettes à la ciboule | 葱油饼
@ Vegetarian | China | 45 | File:Plate of scallion pancakes.jpg | Side
- Plain Flour: 300g
- Boiling Water: 120ml
- Cold Water: 60ml
- Spring Onions: 6
- Vegetable Oil: 5 tbs
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Rice Vinegar: 1 tbs
1. Mix the flour with the boiling water, then the cold water, and knead into a soft dough. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Divide into 4, roll each piece thin, brush with oil, sprinkle with salt and sliced spring onions.
3. Roll up like a cigar, coil into a snail and flatten again to 5mm.
4. Fry each pancake in oil for 3 minutes per side until crisp and golden.
5. Cut into wedges and serve with soy sauce mixed with vinegar.

# Lanzhou beef noodle soup | Soupe de nouilles au bœuf de Lanzhou | 兰州牛肉面
@ Beef | China | 120 | en:Lanzhou beef lamian | Soup
- Beef Shin: 600g
- Daikon Radish: 300g
- Ginger: 30g
- Star Anise: 2
- Cinnamon Stick: 1
- Sichuan Pepper: 1 tsp
- Egg Noodles: 400g
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Spring Onions: 3
- Chilli Oil: 4 tbs
- Salt: 2 tsp
1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes, then simmer it in 2.5 litres of water with the ginger, star anise, cinnamon and Sichuan pepper for 1 hour 30.
2. Remove the beef, slice it thinly and strain the broth. Season with salt.
3. Add the sliced radish to the broth and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Cook the noodles and divide between bowls.
5. Pour over the broth and radish, top with beef, coriander, spring onions and chilli oil.

- Shaoxing Wine: 3 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Egg Noodles: 400g

# Steamed pork buns | Brioches vapeur au porc (baozi) | 肉包子
@ Pork | China | 120 | en:Baozi | Family
- Plain Flour: 500g
- Yeast: 7g
- Warm Water: 260ml
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Minced Pork: 500g
- Spring Onions: 4
- Ginger: 1 tbs grated
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Oyster Sauce: 1 tbs
- Sesame Seed Oil: 1 tbs
1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and warm water and knead for 10 minutes. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
2. Mix the pork with the spring onions, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil and 3 tbs water.
3. Divide the dough into 16 balls, roll each into a disc, fill and pleat closed.
4. Leave to rise for 15 minutes on squares of baking paper.
5. Steam for 15 minutes, then leave the lid on for 3 minutes before opening.
