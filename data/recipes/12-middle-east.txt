# Baked kibbeh | Kibbé au four (boulgour et agneau) | 烤羊肉碎麦饼
@ Lamb | Lebanon | 70 | en:Kibbeh | Family
- Bulgur Wheat: 250g
- Lamb Mince: 700g
- Onions: 2
- Pine Nuts: 50g
- Allspice: 2 tsp
- Ground Cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Olive Oil: 5 tbs
- Yogurt: 300g
- Cucumber: 1
- Mint: 1 handful
1. Soak the bulgur for 15 minutes and squeeze dry.
2. Blend 450g lamb with one onion, the bulgur, half the spices and salt to a paste.
3. Fry the remaining lamb with the other onion, pine nuts and spices for the filling.
4. Press half the paste in an oiled dish, add the filling and cover with the remaining paste. Score into diamonds and drizzle with oil.
5. Bake at 200°C for 35 minutes. Serve with yogurt mixed with cucumber and mint.

# Lamb shish kebab | Brochettes d'agneau (chich kebab) | 羊肉串
@ Lamb | Lebanon | 40 | en:Shish kebab
- Lamb Leg: 800g
- Onions: 1
- Green Pepper: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Lemon: 1
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Paprika: 2 tsp
- Pita Bread: 4
- Tomatoes: 2
- Parsley: 1 bunch
- Yogurt: 200g
1. Marinate the lamb cubes with the garlic, lemon juice, oil and spices for at least 1 hour.
2. Thread onto skewers with pieces of onion and pepper.
3. Grill for 10 minutes, turning, until charred outside and pink inside.
4. Serve with warm pita, tomatoes, parsley and yogurt.

# Maqluba | Maqluba (riz renversé au poulet et aubergines) | 倒扣饭
@ Chicken | Palestine | 90 | en:Maqluba | Rice,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 800g
- Basmati Rice: 400g
- Aubergine: 2
- Cauliflower: 1/2
- Tomatoes: 2
- Onions: 1
- Allspice: 2 tsp
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Ground Cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Chicken Stock: 900ml
- Vegetable Oil: 6 tbs
- Pine Nuts: 40g
- Yogurt: 300g
1. Simmer the chicken with the onion, half the spices and the stock for 25 minutes.
2. Fry or roast the sliced aubergines and cauliflower florets until golden.
3. Layer tomato slices, chicken, vegetables and the rinsed rice mixed with the remaining spices in a deep pot.
4. Pour over the hot strained stock, cover and cook over low heat for 35 minutes.
5. Rest 10 minutes, turn out onto a large plate and top with toasted pine nuts. Serve with yogurt.

# Mansaf | Mansaf (agneau au yaourt et riz) | 约旦酸奶羊肉饭
@ Lamb | Jordan | 120 | en:Mansaf | Rice,Family
- Lamb Shoulder: 1kg
- Greek Yogurt: 600g
- Basmati Rice: 400g
- Onions: 1
- Cardamom: 4
- Bay Leaf: 2
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Ghee: 3 tbs
- Almonds: 50g
- Pine Nuts: 30g
- Pita Bread: 2
- Cornstarch: 1 tbs
1. Simmer the lamb with the onion, cardamom and bay leaves in water for 1 hour 30.
2. Whisk the yogurt with the cornstarch and 500ml of the strained broth, then simmer gently, stirring, for 10 minutes.

5. Lay the bread on a platter, top with rice, lamb and toasted nuts. Serve the sauce on the side.

- Basmati Rice: 300g
1. Peel the aubergines in stripes and fry them whole until golden.
2. Fry the lamb with the onion, pine nuts and allspice.
3. Slit the aubergines lengthways and fill with the meat.

# Lebanese lentil soup | Soupe de lentilles libanaise | 黎巴嫩扁豆汤
@ Vegan | Lebanon | 40 | en:Lentil soup | Soup
- Lentils: 300g
- Onions: 2
- Carrots: 2
- Potatoes: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Ground Cumin: 2 tsp
- Vegetable Stock: 1.5l
- Lemon: 2
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Pita Bread: 4
1. Fry the onions, carrots and garlic in the oil, add the cumin.
2. Add the red lentils, diced potato and stock and simmer for 25 minutes.
3. Blend until smooth and season.
4. Serve with lemon juice and toasted pita.
