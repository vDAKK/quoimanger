# Banh xeo | Bánh xèo (crêpes croustillantes vietnamiennes) | 越南煎饼
@ Pork | Vietnam | 40 | en:Bánh xèo
- Rice Flour: 200g
- Coconut Milk: 200ml
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Spring Onions: 3
- Pork Belly Slices: 250g
- Prawns: 200g
- Bean Sprouts: 200g
- Lettuce: 1
- Mint: 1 handful
- Fish Sauce: 4 tbs
- Lime: 2
- Sugar: 2 tbs
1. Whisk the rice flour, coconut milk, turmeric, sliced spring onions and 300ml water.
2. Fry a few slices of pork and prawns in a hot oiled pan.
3. Pour in a thin layer of batter, add bean sprouts, cover and cook until crisp.
4. Fold in half. Mix the fish sauce with lime juice, sugar and water for the dipping sauce.
5. Wrap pieces in lettuce with mint and dip.

# Bun cha | Bún chả (porc grillé et vermicelles) | 越南烤肉米线
@ Pork | Vietnam | 45 | en:Bún chả
- Minced Pork: 400g
- Pork Belly Slices: 300g
- Fish Sauce: 6 tbs
- Sugar: 4 tbs
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Shallots: 2
- Rice Vermicelli: 300g
- Lime: 2
- Carrots: 1
- Lettuce: 1
- Mint: 1 handful
1. Marinate the minced pork (shaped into patties) and sliced belly with 2 tbs fish sauce, 2 tbs sugar, garlic and shallots.
2. Grill until caramelised.
3. Mix the remaining fish sauce and sugar with lime juice, 250ml warm water and thin carrot slices.
4. Put the meat in the sauce bowls and serve with vermicelli, lettuce and herbs.

# Kuy teav | Kuy teav (soupe de nouilles cambodgienne) | 柬埔寨粿条汤
@ Pork | Cambodia | 60 | en:Kuy teav | Soup
- Pork Back Ribs: 600g
- Minced Pork: 200g
- Prawns: 200g
- Rice Stick Noodles: 400g
- Dried Shrimp: 2 tbs
- Onion: 1
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Bean Sprouts: 150g
- Lime: 2
1. Simmer the ribs with the onion and dried shrimp in 2.5 litres of water for 40 minutes.
2. Season with fish sauce and sugar. Cook the minced pork and prawns in the broth.
3. Fry the garlic until crisp.
4. Serve the noodles in the broth with the meat, garlic oil, bean sprouts and lime.

- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Dill: 1 bunch
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Spinach: 150g

# Khao piak sen | Khao piak sen (soupe de nouilles laotienne) | 老挝米粉汤
@ Chicken | Laos | 60 | en:Khao piak sen | Soup
- Chicken Legs: 4
- Rice Flour: 200g
- Plain Flour: 150g
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Shallots: 3
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Bean Sprouts: 150g
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Spring Onions: 3
- Lime: 2
1. Simmer the chicken in 2.5 litres of water for 40 minutes, then shred it.
2. Knead the flours with hot water, roll thick and cut into noodles.
3. Cook the noodles directly in the broth so it thickens.
4. Season with fish sauce. Serve with chicken, fried garlic and shallots, herbs and lime.
