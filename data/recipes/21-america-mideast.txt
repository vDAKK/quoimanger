# Chicken and sausage gumbo | Gumbo poulet et saucisse | 秋葵浓汤
@ Chicken | United States | 90 | en:Gumbo | Stew,Spicy
- Chicken Thighs: 6
- Kielbasa: 300g
- Plain Flour: 80g
- Vegetable Oil: 80ml
- Onions: 1
- Green Pepper: 1
- Celery: 3 sticks
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Okra: 200g
- Cajun: 1 tbs
- Chicken Stock: 1.2 litres
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Spring Onions: 3
- Rice: 300g
1. Cook the flour in the oil, stirring constantly, for 15 to 20 minutes until the roux is chocolate brown.
2. Add the onion, pepper and celery and cook 5 minutes, then the garlic and cajun spices.
3. Whisk in the stock. Add the chicken, sliced sausage, okra and bay leaves and simmer for 45 minutes.
4. Shred the chicken. Serve over rice with spring onions.

# Classic meatloaf | Pain de viande à l’américaine | 美式肉饼

- Breadcrumbs: 60g
- Milk: 100ml
- Eggs: 2
- Worcestershire Sauce: 1 tbs
- Tomato Ketchup: 6 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 1 tbs
- Cider Vinegar: 1 tbs
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Butter: 40g
1. Mix the meats with the grated onion, breadcrumbs soaked in milk, eggs, Worcestershire sauce and salt.
2. Shape into a loaf on a baking tray.
3. Mix the ketchup, sugar and vinegar and brush over. Bake at 180°C for 1 hour.

- Hotsauce: 120ml
- Butter: 60g
- Celery: 4 sticks
- Carrots: 3
- Stilton Cheese: 80g
- Sour Cream: 150ml
- Mayonnaise: 3 tbs
1. Toss the wings with baking powder and salt. Bake on a rack at 220°C for 45 minutes, turning once.

# Shrimp and grits | Crevettes et polenta à la cajun | 虾配玉米粥
@ Seafood | United States | 35 | en:Shrimp and grits | Shellfish,Quick
- King Prawns: 500g
- Cornmeal: 200g
- Milk: 500ml
- Cheddar Cheese: 100g
- Butter: 40g
- Bacon: 4 slices
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Cajun: 2 tsp
- Lemon: 1
- Spring Onions: 3
1. Bring the milk and 500ml water to a boil, whisk in the cornmeal and cook 15 minutes, stirring. Add the cheese and half the butter.
2. Fry the bacon until crisp and crumble it.
3. Cook the prawns in the bacon fat with garlic, cajun spice and the remaining butter for 3 minutes. Add the lemon juice.
4. Serve the prawns on the grits with bacon and spring onions.

# BBQ pork ribs | Travers de porc barbecue | 烧烤猪肋排
@ Pork | United States | 180 | en:Pork ribs | Barbecue,Grill
- Pork Back Ribs: 2kg
- Brown Sugar: 3 tbs
- Smoked Paprika: 2 tbs
- Garlic Powder: 1 tsp
- Mustard Powder: 1 tsp
- Barbeque Sauce: 250ml
- White Cabbage: 0.5
- Carrots: 2
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbs
- Cider Vinegar: 2 tbs
- Corn On The Cob: 4
1. Rub the ribs with sugar, paprika, garlic, mustard and salt. Wrap tightly in foil.
2. Bake at 150°C for 2 h 30.

# Chicken and dumplings | Poulet et boulettes de pâte | 美式鸡肉团子汤
@ Chicken | United States | 70 | en:Chicken and dumplings | Soup,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Onions: 1
- Carrots: 3
- Celery: 2 sticks
- Chicken Stock: 1.5 litres
- Thyme: 2 sprigs
- Plain Flour: 250g
- Baking Powder: 2 tsp
- Butter: 50g
- Milk: 180ml
- Parsley: 1 handful
1. Simmer the chicken with the vegetables, thyme and stock for 35 minutes. Shred the chicken.
2. Rub the butter into the flour, baking powder and salt, then stir in the milk to a soft dough.
3. Drop spoonfuls of dough into the simmering soup.
4. Cover and cook 15 minutes without lifting the lid. Return the chicken and add parsley.

# Chickpea fatteh | Fatteh aux pois chiches | 鹰嘴豆酸奶面包
@ Vegetarian | Lebanon | 30 | en:Fatteh | Vegetarian,Quick
- Pita Bread: 4
- Chickpeas: 800g
- Greek Yogurt: 500g
- Tahini: 3 tbs
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Lemon: 1
- Pine Nuts: 40g
- Butter: 30g
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Paprika: 1 tsp
1. Cut the pita into squares, brush with oil and bake at 200°C for 10 minutes until crisp.
2. Warm the chickpeas in their liquid with the cumin.
3. Whisk the yogurt with tahini, crushed garlic, lemon juice and salt.
4. Layer bread, chickpeas and yogurt. Top with pine nuts fried in butter, paprika and parsley.

# Shish barak | Shish barak (raviolis à l’agneau, sauce yaourt) | 黎巴嫩酸奶羊肉饺
@ Lamb | Lebanon | 90 | en:Shish barak | Dumplings
- Plain Flour: 300g
- Lamb Mince: 300g
- Onions: 1
- Pine Nuts: 40g
- Ground Allspice: 0.5 tsp
- Greek Yogurt: 800g
- Corn Flour: 1 tbs
- Eggs: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Butter: 30g
1. Make a dough with the flour, 150ml water and salt. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Fry the lamb with onion, pine nuts and allspice.
3. Roll the dough thin, cut small circles, fill, fold and pinch the ends together. Bake at 200°C for 10 minutes.
4. Heat the yogurt with the corn flour and egg, stirring constantly. Add the dumplings and simmer 10 minutes. Top with garlic and coriander fried in butter.

# Mloukhieh with chicken | Mloukhieh au poulet | 黎巴嫩黄麻叶炖鸡
@ Chicken | Lebanon | 75 | en:Mulukhiyah | Stew
- Chicken Legs: 4
- Mulukhiyah: 400g
- Garlic: 8 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Onions: 1
- Cinnamon Stick: 1
- Lemon: 2
- Chicken Stock: 1 litre
- Vermicelli Pasta: 50g
- Rice: 300g
1. Simmer the chicken with the onion and cinnamon in the stock for 40 minutes. Shred it.
2. Fry the crushed garlic and chopped coriander in oil until fragrant.
3. Add the chopped mulukhiyah leaves and the broth and simmer 15 minutes. Return the chicken.
4. Fry the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and cook it. Serve with the stew and lemon.

# Joojeh kabab | Joojeh kabab (brochettes de poulet au safran) | 伊朗藏红花烤鸡串
@ Chicken | Iran | 40 | File:Joojeh-kabab.JPG | Grill,Rice
- Chicken Breasts: 4
- Saffron: 1 pinch
- Greek Yogurt: 200g
- Onions: 1
- Lemon: 2
- Butter: 30g
- Basmati Rice: 400g
- Tomatoes: 4
- Sumac: 1 tsp
1. Steep the saffron in 2 tbs of hot water. Mix with the yogurt, grated onion, lemon juice and salt. Marinate the chicken pieces for at least 1 hour.
2. Thread onto skewers with tomato halves.
3. Grill for 10 to 12 minutes, basting with melted butter.
4. Serve on rice with sumac.

# Mirza ghasemi | Mirza ghasemi (aubergines fumées aux œufs) | 伊朗烟熏茄子炒蛋
@ Vegetarian | Iran | 50 | File:Mirza-Qasemi dish.jpg | Vegetarian,Eggs
- Aubergine: 4
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Tomatoes: 4
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Eggs: 4
- Vegetable Oil: 4 tbs
- Pita Bread: 4
1. Grill the whole aubergines over a flame or under the grill until blackened and soft. Peel and chop.
2. Fry the garlic in oil with the turmeric, then add the chopped tomatoes and cook 10 minutes.
3. Add the aubergines and cook 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the beaten eggs until just set. Serve with bread.
