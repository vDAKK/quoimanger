# Oyakodon | Oyakodon (bol de riz poulet et œuf) | 亲子丼
@ Chicken | Japan | 25 | en:Oyakodon | Quick,Rice
- Chicken Thighs: 4
- Onion: 1
- Eggs: 6
- Soy Sauce: 4 tbs
- Mirin: 3 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Water: 200ml
- Sushi Rice: 320g
- Spring Onions: 2
1. Cook the rice.
2. Simmer the water, soy sauce, mirin and sugar in a frying pan. Add the sliced onion and cook for 3 minutes.
3. Add the chicken cut into bite-size pieces and simmer for 7 minutes.
4. Pour over the lightly beaten eggs, cover and cook for 1 minute until just set.
5. Slide onto bowls of rice and top with sliced spring onions.

# Japanese chicken curry | Curry japonais au poulet | 日式咖喱鸡饭
@ Chicken | Japan | 50 | en:Japanese curry | Curry,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 600g
- Onions: 2
- Carrots: 2
- Potatoes: 3
- Curry Powder: 2 tbs
- Plain Flour: 3 tbs
- Butter: 40g
- Chicken Stock: 800ml
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Honey: 1 tbs
- Sushi Rice: 320g
1. Brown the chicken pieces in a little oil. Add the onions, carrots and potatoes in chunks and cook for 5 minutes.
2. Add the stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Meanwhile melt the butter, stir in the flour and curry powder and cook for 3 minutes to make a roux.
4. Whisk a ladle of stock into the roux, then stir it into the pot with the soy sauce and honey. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
5. Serve with the rice.

# Okonomiyaki | Okonomiyaki (galette japonaise au chou) | 大阪烧
@ Pork | Japan | 30 | en:Okonomiyaki | Pancake
- Plain Flour: 150g
- Eggs: 4
- Water: 150ml
- White Cabbage: 400g
- Spring Onions: 4
- Streaky Bacon: 8 slices
- Mayonnaise: 4 tbs
- Tomato Ketchup: 3 tbs
- Worcestershire Sauce: 2 tbs
1. Whisk the flour, eggs and water into a batter. Fold in the finely shredded cabbage and sliced spring onions.
2. Pour a quarter of the batter into an oiled frying pan, shape into a thick round and lay 2 bacon slices on top.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, flip and cook for 5 minutes more until the bacon is crisp. Repeat.
4. Mix the ketchup and Worcestershire sauce, brush over the pancakes and drizzle with mayonnaise.

# Red braised pork belly | Porc braisé rouge (hong shao rou) | 红烧肉
@ Pork | China | 90 | en:Red braised pork belly | Braise

- Brown Sugar: 2 tbs
- Shaoxing Wine: 3 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Dark Soy Sauce: 1 tbs
- Ginger: 4 slices
- Star Anise: 2
- Spring Onions: 3
- Rice: 300g
1. Cut the pork into cubes, blanch in boiling water for 3 minutes and drain.
2. Melt the sugar in a wok until amber, add the pork and toss to coat.
3. Add the wine, both soy sauces, ginger, star anise, spring onions and water to just cover. Simmer covered for 1 hour.
4. Uncover and boil for 10 minutes until the sauce is sticky. Serve with rice.
