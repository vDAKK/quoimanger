# Rajma | Rajma (curry de haricots rouges) | 印式红芸豆咖喱
@ Vegetarian | India | 45 | en:Rajma | Curry,Family
- Kidney Beans: 800g
- Onions: 2
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Ginger Garlic Paste: 2 tbs
- Green Chilli: 2
- Cumin Seeds: 1 tsp
- Ground Coriander: 2 tsp
- Ground Turmeric: 1/2 tsp
- Chilli Powder: 1 tsp
- Garam Masala: 1 tsp
- Ghee: 2 tbs
- Basmati Rice: 300g
- Coriander Leaves: 1 handful
1. Cook the rice. Fry the cumin seeds in the ghee, then the chopped onions until golden.
2. Add the ginger garlic paste and chillies, then the ground spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes until the oil separates.
4. Add the drained beans and 400ml water, simmer for 20 minutes and mash a few beans to thicken.
5. Finish with garam masala and coriander and serve with rice.

# Chole bhature | Chole bhature (pois chiches et pains frits) | 鹰嘴豆配炸面包
@ Vegetarian | India | 60 | en:Chole bhature | Curry
- Chickpeas: 800g
- Onions: 2
- Tomatoes: 3
- Ginger Garlic Paste: 1 tbs
- Garam Masala: 2 tsp
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Ground Coriander: 2 tsp
- Chilli Powder: 1 tsp
- Plain Flour: 300g
- Yogurt: 100g
- Baking Powder: 1 tsp
- Vegetable Oil: 500ml
1. Mix the flour, yogurt, baking powder, a pinch of salt and enough water to make a soft dough. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Fry the onions until brown, add the ginger garlic paste, spices and chopped tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Add the chickpeas and 300ml water and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Roll the dough into 8 discs and deep-fry until they puff up.
5. Serve the chickpeas with the hot breads.

# Pav bhaji | Pav bhaji (purée de légumes épicée et petits pains) | 孟买蔬菜糊配面包
@ Vegetarian | India | 45 | en:Pav bhaji | Family
- Potatoes: 3
- Cauliflower: 1/2
- Frozen Peas: 100g
- Green Pepper: 1
- Onions: 2
- Tomatoes: 4
- Ginger Garlic Paste: 1 tbs
- Garam Masala: 2 tsp
- Chilli Powder: 1 tsp
- Butter: 80g
- Bread Rolls: 8
- Lemon: 1
- Coriander Leaves: 1 handful
1. Boil the potatoes, cauliflower and peas until soft, then drain and mash.
2. Melt half the butter, fry one onion and the pepper, add the ginger garlic paste and tomatoes and cook for 8 minutes.
3. Add the spices and the mashed vegetables with a little water and mash together for 10 minutes.
4. Toast the halved rolls in the remaining butter.
5. Serve the bhaji with the rolls, chopped raw onion, lemon wedges and coriander.

# Aloo paratha | Aloo paratha (galettes farcies aux pommes de terre) | 土豆馅饼
@ Vegetarian | India | 50 | en:Aloo paratha
- Plain Flour: 300g
- Potatoes: 4
- Green Chilli: 2
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Garam Masala: 1 tsp
- Coriander Leaves: 1 handful
- Ghee: 4 tbs
- Yogurt: 300g
- Salt: 1 tsp
1. Knead the flour with salt and about 180ml water into a soft dough and rest for 20 minutes.
2. Boil and mash the potatoes with the chilli, spices, coriander and salt.
3. Roll a ball of dough, fill it with potato, close it and roll out again gently.
4. Cook in a hot pan, brushing with ghee, for 2 minutes per side until golden.
5. Serve with yogurt.
