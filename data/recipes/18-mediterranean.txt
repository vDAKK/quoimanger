# Bosnian pot | Bosanski lonac (ragoût bosnien) | 波斯尼亚炖锅
@ Beef | Bosnia and Herzegovina | 150 | en:Bosanski lonac | Stew,Family
- Beef: 500g
- Lamb Shoulder: 400g
- Potatoes: 4
- Carrots: 2
- White Cabbage: 0.5
- Onions: 2
- Tomatoes: 3
- Green Beans: 200g
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Peppercorns: 1 tsp
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Parsley: 1 handful
1. Layer the meat cubes with all the vegetables in a deep pot, seasoning each layer with salt, garlic and peppercorns.
2. Add the bay leaves and water to come halfway up.
3. Cover tightly and simmer very gently (or bake at 160°C) for 2 h 30 without stirring.
4. Sprinkle with parsley.

# Serbian pljeskavica | Pljeskavica (burger serbe) | 塞尔维亚肉饼
@ Beef | Serbia | 35 | en:Pljeskavica | Burger,Grill,Quick
- Minced Beef: 500g
- Minced Pork: 300g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Pita Bread: 4
- Feta: 150g
- Tomatoes: 2
- Cucumber: 1
- Red Pepper: 1
1. Mix the meats with one grated onion, garlic, paprika and salt. Shape 4 large thin patties.
2. Grill for 4 minutes per side.
3. Dice the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper and other onion with crumbled feta for a šopska-style salad.
4. Serve the patties in warm pita with the salad.

# Serbian stuffed peppers | Poivrons farcis serbes | 塞尔维亚酿甜椒
@ Beef | Serbia | 80 | en:Stuffed peppers | Bake,Family
- Yellow Pepper: 8
- Minced Beef: 500g
- Rice: 120g
- Onions: 1
- Paprika: 2 tsp
- Eggs: 1
- Passata: 500ml
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Sour Cream: 150ml
1. Fry the onion, add the beef and paprika. Off the heat mix in the raw rice, egg and salt.
2. Cut the tops off the peppers, remove the seeds and fill three-quarters full.
3. Stand them in a pot, add the passata thickened with flour and water to half their height.
4. Simmer covered for 1 hour. Serve with sour cream.

# Croatian black risotto | Risotto noir croate | 克罗地亚墨鱼汁烩饭
@ Seafood | Croatia | 50 | File:Risotto nero di seppia.jpg | Rice,Shellfish
- Squid: 600g
- Arborio Rice: 320g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Dry White Wine: 150ml
- Fish Stock: 1.2 litres
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Lemon: 1
1. Cut the squid into rings and keep the ink sachets.
2. Fry the onion and garlic in oil, add the squid and cook 10 minutes.
3. Add the rice, wine, tomato puree and ink, then the hot stock a ladle at a time for 18 minutes.
4. Finish with parsley and lemon.

# Pasticada | Pašticada (bœuf braisé dalmate) | 达尔马提亚炖牛肉
@ Beef | Croatia | 180 | en:Pašticada | Braise,Classic
- Beef: 1.2kg
- Streaky Bacon: 100g
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Red Wine Vinegar: 150ml
- Onions: 2
- Carrots: 2
- Celeriac: 0.25
- Prunes: 10
- Red Wine: 300ml
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Cloves: 3
- Gnocchi: 800g
1. Lard the beef with bacon and garlic and marinate in the vinegar overnight.
2. Brown the beef, then the onions and vegetables. Add the prunes, cloves, tomato puree and wine.
3. Braise covered on low heat for 2 h 30, adding water as needed.
4. Blend the sauce. Serve the sliced beef with the sauce and gnocchi.

# Greek stifado | Stifado (bœuf aux petits oignons) | 希腊洋葱炖牛肉
@ Beef | Greece | 150 | en:Stifado | Stew,Classic
- Beef: 1kg
- Shallots: 600g
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Red Wine: 250ml
- Red Wine Vinegar: 2 tbs
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Tomato Puree: 2 tbs
- Cinnamon Stick: 1
- Cloves: 4
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Orzo: 300g
1. Brown the beef in oil. Add the garlic, wine and vinegar.
2. Add the tomatoes, tomato puree, spices and enough water to cover. Simmer 1 hour.
3. Add the peeled whole shallots and cook 1 hour more until thick.
4. Serve with orzo or bread.

# Greek gemista | Gemista (légumes farcis au riz) | 希腊酿蔬菜
@ Vegan | Greece | 90 | en:Gemista | Vegetarian,Bake
- Tomatoes: 6
- Green Pepper: 4
- Rice: 200g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Mint: 1 handful
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Olive Oil: 100ml
- Potatoes: 4
1. Hollow out the tomatoes and peppers. Chop the tomato flesh.
2. Fry the onion and garlic, add the rice, tomato flesh, herbs and salt. Cook 5 minutes.
3. Fill the vegetables, put on the lids and set in a dish with potato wedges. Drizzle with oil.
4. Add a glass of water and bake at 180°C for 1 h 15.

# Greek fasolada | Fasolada (soupe de haricots grecque) | 希腊白豆汤
@ Vegan | Greece | 60 | en:Fasolada | Soup,Vegetarian
- Cannellini Beans: 800g
- Carrots: 2
- Celery: 2 sticks
- Onions: 1
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Olive Oil: 100ml
- Bay Leaves: 2
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Soften the onion, carrots and celery in the oil.
2. Add the tomatoes, tomato puree, bay leaves, drained beans and 1 litre water.
3. Simmer 40 minutes until thick.
4. Add parsley and serve with bread.

# Turkish iskender kebab | İskender kebab | 土耳其伊斯坎德尔烤肉
@ Lamb | Turkey | 40 | en:İskender kebap | Classic
- Lamb Leg: 600g
- Pita Bread: 4
- Passata: 300ml
- Butter: 60g
- Greek Yogurt: 400g
- Paprika: 1 tsp
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Green Pepper: 2
- Tomatoes: 2
1. Marinate thin lamb slices with cumin, paprika, garlic and salt. Sear quickly in a hot pan.
2. Warm the passata with a little butter and salt.
3. Cut the pita into squares on plates, top with the meat and tomato sauce.
4. Pour over sizzling melted butter, add the yogurt and grilled peppers and tomatoes.

# Turkish manti | Mantı (raviolis turcs au yaourt) | 土耳其小饺子
@ Lamb | Turkey | 90 | en:Manti (food) | Dumplings
- Plain Flour: 400g
- Eggs: 1
- Lamb Mince: 300g
- Onions: 1
- Greek Yogurt: 400g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Butter: 50g
- Pul Biber: 2 tsp
- Dried Mint: 1 tsp
1. Knead the flour, egg, 150ml water and salt. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Mix the lamb with grated onion and salt.
3. Roll the dough thinly, cut into 4cm squares, add a little filling and pinch the corners together.
4. Boil for 10 minutes. Serve with garlic yogurt and butter melted with pul biber and mint.

# Spanish albondigas | Albóndigas (boulettes à la tomate) | 西班牙番茄肉丸
@ Beef | Spain | 45 | en:Albóndigas | Family
- Minced Beef: 300g
- Minced Pork: 300g
- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Eggs: 1
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Onions: 1
- Chopped Tomatoes: 400g
- Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp
- Dry White Wine: 100ml
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Rice: 300g
1. Mix the meats with breadcrumbs, egg, garlic, parsley and salt. Roll into balls and brown in oil.
2. Fry the onion in the same pan, add the paprika, wine and tomatoes.

4. Serve with rice or bread.

# Portuguese caldo verde | Caldo verde | 葡萄牙羽衣甘蓝汤
@ Pork | Portugal | 40 | en:Caldo verde | Soup,Quick
- Potatoes: 800g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Kale: 250g
- Chorizo: 150g
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Simmer the potatoes, onion and garlic in 1.5 litres salted water for 20 minutes. Mash or blend.
2. Shred the kale very finely and add to the soup for 5 minutes.
3. Fry the chorizo slices.
4. Serve with chorizo, a drizzle of olive oil and bread.

# Portuguese bacalhau a bras | Bacalhau à Brás | 葡萄牙土豆丝炒鳕鱼
@ Seafood | Portugal | 40 | en:Bacalhau à Brás | Fish,Eggs
- Salt Cod: 400g
- Potatoes: 600g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Eggs: 6
- Black Olives: 60g
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Olive Oil: 5 tbs
- Sunflower Oil: 300ml
1. Soak the cod for 24 hours, changing the water. Flake it.
2. Cut the potatoes into matchsticks and fry until crisp.
3. Fry the sliced onions and garlic in olive oil, add the cod and cook 5 minutes.
4. Add the fries and the beaten eggs and stir until just creamy. Top with olives and parsley.
