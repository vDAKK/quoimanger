# Pad pak bung | Pad pak bung (liseron d’eau sauté) | 泰式炒空心菜
@ Vegan | Thailand | 15 | en:Ipomoea aquatica | Side,Quick,Stir-fry
- Morning Glory: 500g
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Birds-eye Chillies: 3
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Fermented Black Beans: 1 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Vegetable Oil: 2 tbs
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cut the morning glory into 8cm lengths.
2. Crush the garlic and chillies.
3. Stir-fry everything in very hot oil for 1 minute with the black beans, soy sauce, sugar and a splash of water.
4. Serve immediately with rice.

# Kai jeow | Kai jeow (omelette thaïe) | 泰式煎蛋饼
@ Pork | Thailand | 15 | File:Khao khaichiao (omelette with rice), Bangkok, 2018-05-15.jpg | Eggs,Quick
- Eggs: 6
- Minced Pork: 150g
- Fish Sauce: 1 tbs
- Lime: 1
- Spring Onions: 2
- Sriracha: 2 tbs
- Vegetable Oil: 200ml
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Beat the eggs with the pork, fish sauce, lime juice and sliced spring onions.
2. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking.
3. Pour in the eggs from a height: they puff up. Turn after 1 minute and cook 1 minute more.
4. Drain and serve on rice with sriracha.

# Pad woon sen | Pad woon sen (vermicelles sautées) | 泰式炒粉丝
@ Chicken | Thailand | 20 | File:Fried cellophane noodles with shrimp Pad woon sen kung.jpg | Noodles,Quick,Stir-fry
- Glass Noodles: 200g
- Chicken Breasts: 2
- Eggs: 2
- Cabbage: 0.25
- Carrots: 1
- Onions: 1
- Tomatoes: 2
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Oyster Sauce: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Spring Onions: 3
- Vegetable Oil: 3 tbs
1. Soak the noodles in warm water for 10 minutes and drain.
2. Stir-fry the garlic and sliced chicken until cooked. Push aside and scramble the eggs.
3. Add the vegetables and stir-fry 2 minutes.
4. Add the noodles, sauces, sugar and a splash of water. Toss until glossy. Add the spring onions.

# Pineapple fried rice | Riz sauté à l’ananas | 菠萝炒饭
@ Seafood | Thailand | 25 | File:Fried Rice in Pineapple.jpg | Rice,Quick
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
- Pineapple Chunks: 250g
- Prawns: 250g
- Eggs: 2
- Cashew Nuts: 50g
- Onions: 1
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Curry Powder: 1 tsp
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Raisins: 30g
- Spring Onions: 3
- Vegetable Oil: 3 tbs
1. Cook the rice the day before or let it cool completely.
2. Stir-fry the onion, garlic and prawns. Push aside and scramble the eggs.
3. Add the rice, curry powder and fish sauce and toss on high heat.
4. Add the pineapple, raisins and cashews and heat through. Top with spring onions.

# Pla rad prik | Pla rad prik (poisson frit sauce pimentée) | 泰式辣酱炸鱼
@ Seafood | Thailand | 35 | File:DFC 2100 Spicy Thai-style fish topped with omelet fresh chilies garlic shallots and cilantro served with cucumber and lime.jpg | Fish,Fried,Spicy
- White Fish Fillets: 4
- Corn Flour: 4 tbs
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Red Chilli: 4
- Shallots: 3
- Palm Sugar: 3 tbs
- Tamarind Paste: 2 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Vegetable Oil: 300ml
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Dust the fish with corn flour and fry until crisp. Drain.
2. Pound the garlic, chillies and shallots and fry in 2 tbs of oil.
3. Add the palm sugar, tamarind and fish sauce and simmer into a thick sauce.
4. Pour over the fish, add fried basil and serve with rice.

# Yam woon sen | Yam woon sen (salade de vermicelles) | 泰式凉拌粉丝

# Boat noodles | Kuay teow reua (nouilles des bateaux) | 泰式船面
@ Beef | Thailand | 90 | en:Boat noodles | Noodles,Soup
- Beef Brisket: 500g
- Beef Stock: 2 litres
- Star Anise: 3
- Cinnamon Stick: 1
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Dark Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Rice Noodles: 300g
- Bean Sprouts: 200g
- Morning Glory: 200g
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Dried Chillies: 2 tsp
1. Simmer the brisket in the stock with the spices, garlic and soy sauces for 1 h 15. Slice it.

# Hoy tod | Hoy tod (galette croustillante aux moules) | 泰式蚝煎
@ Seafood | Thailand | 25 | en:Oyster omelette | Eggs,Shellfish,Quick
- Mussels: 600g
- Rice Flour: 80g
- Potato Starch: 40g
- Eggs: 3
- Bean Sprouts: 200g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Spring Onions: 3
- Sriracha: 3 tbs
- Vegetable Oil: 6 tbs
1. Steam the mussels open and remove from the shells.
2. Mix the flours with 200ml water into a thin batter.
3. Pour the batter into a very hot oiled pan, add the mussels, then crack in the eggs and break them up. Fry until crisp.
4. Serve on quickly fried bean sprouts with garlic, spring onions and sriracha.

# Tod man pla | Tod man pla (galettes de poisson) | 泰式鱼饼
@ Seafood | Thailand | 35 | File:Thanin market tod man pla.jpg | Fish,Fried,Starter
- White Fish Fillets: 500g
- Red Curry Paste: 2 tbs
- Eggs: 1
- Fish Sauce: 1 tbs
- Kaffir Lime Leaves: 5
- Green Beans: 100g
- Cucumber: 1
- Sweet Chilli Sauce: 6 tbs
- Roasted Peanut: 30g
- Vegetable Oil: 300ml
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Blend the fish, curry paste, egg and fish sauce into a sticky paste.
2. Mix in the thinly sliced lime leaves and green beans.

# Khai palo | Khai palo (œufs et porc aux cinq-épices) | 泰式五香卤蛋猪肉
@ Pork | Thailand | 90 | File:Mu phalo.JPG | Eggs,Braise
- Pork Belly Slices: 600g
- Eggs: 6
- Five Spice Powder: 2 tsp
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Palm Sugar: 3 tbs
- Dark Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Star Anise: 2
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Hard-boil and peel the eggs.
2. Pound the garlic, coriander roots and white pepper and fry with the palm sugar until caramelised.
3. Add the pork and brown, then the five spice, soy sauces, star anise and 1 litre of water.
4. Add the eggs and simmer for 1 hour. Serve with rice.

# Pad kana moo krob | Brocoli chinois au porc croustillant | 芥兰炒脆皮猪肉
@ Pork | Thailand | 70 | File:Phat khana mu krop.jpg | Stir-fry
- Pork Belly Slices: 500g
- Chinese Broccoli: 400g
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Red Chilli: 2
- Oyster Sauce: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 1 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Vegetable Oil: 200ml
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Simmer the pork belly for 20 minutes, dry it well, then roast at 220°C for 30 minutes until crackling.
2. Cut into bite-size pieces.
3. Stir-fry the garlic and chilli, then the sliced broccoli with a splash of water.
4. Add the pork and sauces and toss. Serve with rice.

# Gai tod | Gai tod (poulet frit thaï) | 泰式炸鸡
@ Chicken | Thailand | 60 | File:Kai thot krueng thae.JPG | Fried
- Chicken Drumsticks: 10
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- White Pepper: 1 tsp
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Rice Flour: 100g
- Sweet Chilli Sauce: 6 tbs
- Sticky Rice Flour: 2 tbs
- Glutinous Rice: 300g
- Vegetable Oil: 1 litre
1. Pound the garlic, coriander roots and pepper. Marinate the chicken with it and the fish sauce for 30 minutes.
2. Soak and steam the glutinous rice for 25 minutes.
3. Toss the chicken in the mixed flours and fry at 170°C for 12 minutes until golden.
4. Top with fried garlic and serve with sticky rice and sweet chilli sauce.

# Red duck curry with pineapple | Curry rouge de canard à l’ananas | 菠萝红咖喱鸭
@ Miscellaneous | Thailand | 50 | File:Red roast duck curry.jpg | Curry,Spicy
- Duck Breast: 2
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 3 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 800ml
- Pineapple Chunks: 300g
- Cherry Tomatoes: 12
- Kaffir Lime Leaves: 5
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 1 tbs
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the duck skin-side down for 10 minutes, then 3 minutes on the other side. Slice.
2. Fry the curry paste in a little coconut milk until fragrant.
3. Add the rest of the coconut milk, lime leaves, pineapple, tomatoes, fish sauce and sugar. Simmer 10 minutes.
4. Add the duck and basil. Serve with rice.
