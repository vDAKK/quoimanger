# Thai red chicken curry | Curry rouge thaï au poulet | 泰式红咖喱鸡
@ Chicken | Thailand | 30 | en:Red curry | Curry,Spicy
- Chicken Thighs: 600g
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 3 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 400ml
- Green Beans: 200g
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 1 tbs
- Kaffir Lime Leaves: 4
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice.
2. Fry the curry paste in a few spoons of the thick coconut cream for 2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add the sliced chicken and cook for 3 minutes, then the rest of the coconut milk, the lime leaves, fish sauce and sugar.
4. Simmer for 12 minutes, adding the green beans for the last 5 minutes. Stir in the basil and serve with rice.

# Thai yellow curry with chicken and potatoes | Curry jaune thaï poulet pommes de terre | 泰式黄咖喱鸡
@ Chicken | Thailand | 35 | en:Yellow curry | Curry,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 600g
- Curry Powder: 2 tbs
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 1 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 400ml
- Potatoes: 400g
- Onion: 1
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 1 tbs
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice. Fry the curry paste and curry powder in a little oil for 1 minute.
2. Add the chicken pieces and onion and cook for 4 minutes.
3. Add the coconut milk, potatoes in cubes, fish sauce, sugar and 150ml water. Simmer for 20 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
4. Serve with rice.

# Larb gai | Larb gai (salade de poulet haché thaï) | 泰式鸡肉末沙拉
@ Chicken | Thailand | 20 | en:Larb | Quick,Salad,Spicy
- Chicken Breasts: 500g
- Lime: 2
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Red Onions: 1
- Mint: 1 handful
- Coriander: 1 handful
- Chilli Flakes: 1 tsp
- Rice: 2 tbs
- Little Gem Lettuce: 2
1. Toast the 2 tbs of rice in a dry pan until golden, then grind to a powder.
2. Cook the minced chicken with 3 tbs water for 5 minutes, breaking it up.
3. Off the heat, add the lime juice, fish sauce, chilli, sliced onion, herbs and toasted rice powder.
4. Serve in lettuce cups.

# Thai basil tofu stir-fry | Tofu sauté au basilic thaï | 泰式罗勒炒豆腐
@ Vegan | Thailand | 20 | en:Phat kaphrao | Quick,Spicy
- Tofu: 400g
- Green Beans: 150g
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Red Chilli: 2
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Brown Sugar: 1 tsp
- Basil Leaves: 2 handfuls
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice. Crumble or dice the tofu and pat dry.
2. Fry the tofu in a hot wok for 6 minutes until golden.
3. Add the crushed garlic, chilli and green beans in pieces and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
4. Add the soy sauce, sugar and basil and toss until wilted.

# Khao man gai | Khao man gai (riz au poulet poché) | 泰式海南鸡饭
@ Chicken | Thailand | 60 | en:Hainanese chicken rice | Rice
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Jasmine Rice: 320g
- Ginger: 5 slices
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Cucumber: 1
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Rice Vinegar: 2 tbs
- Red Chilli: 1
- Coriander: 1 handful
1. Poach the chicken in water with the ginger and 2 garlic cloves for 25 minutes. Keep the broth.
2. Fry the rice with the remaining chopped garlic for 2 minutes, then cook it in 500ml of the chicken broth for 15 minutes.
3. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, a little ginger and chopped chilli for the sauce.
4. Slice the chicken and serve on the rice with cucumber, coriander, the sauce and a bowl of broth.

# Shaking beef | Bœuf sauté vietnamien (bò lúc lắc) | 越南骰子牛肉

- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Oyster Sauce: 2 tbs
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Red Onions: 1
- Rocket: 100g
- Cherry Tomatoes: 200g
- Lime: 1
- Rice: 300g
1. Cut the steak into 2cm cubes and marinate with the soy sauce, oyster sauce and garlic for 10 minutes.
2. Cook the rice.
3. Sear the beef in a very hot pan in one layer for 1 minute, then shake the pan for 2 minutes more. Add the onion wedges for the last minute.

# Chicken adobo | Adobo de poulet | 菲律宾醋焖鸡
@ Chicken | Philippines | 50 | en:Philippine adobo | Braise
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Soy Sauce: 100ml
- White Wine Vinegar: 100ml
- Garlic: 8 cloves
- Bay Leaves: 3
- Peppercorns: 1 tsp
- Brown Sugar: 1 tbs
- Rice: 300g
1. Put the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, crushed garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns in a pan with 150ml water.
2. Bring to the boil without stirring, then cover and simmer for 30 minutes.

# Butter chicken | Poulet au beurre (butter chicken) | 黄油鸡
@ Chicken | India | 45 | en:Butter chicken | Curry,Family
- Chicken Thighs: 700g
- Greek Yogurt: 150g
- Garam Masala: 2 tsp
- Ginger Garlic Paste: 2 tbs
- Butter: 50g
- Onion: 1
- Passata: 400ml
- Double Cream: 150ml
- Chilli Powder: 1 tsp
- Basmati Rice: 300g
- Naan Bread: 4
1. Marinate the chicken pieces in the yogurt, half the garam masala and half the ginger garlic paste for 20 minutes.
2. Grill or pan-fry the chicken for 10 minutes until charred.
3. Melt the butter, fry the onion for 6 minutes, add the rest of the paste, spices and the passata and simmer for 10 minutes. Blend smooth.
4. Add the chicken and cream and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve with rice and naan.

# Malaysian chicken satay with peanut sauce | Brochettes saté et sauce cacahuète | 马来西亚沙爹鸡串
@ Chicken | Malaysia | 40 | en:Satay | Grill
- Chicken Thighs: 700g
- Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Ground Cumin: 1 tsp
- Brown Sugar: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 3 tbs
- Peanut Butter: 5 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 200ml
- Lime: 1
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
- Cucumber: 1
1. Marinate the chicken strips with the turmeric, cumin, 1 tbs sugar and 2 tbs soy sauce for 20 minutes. Thread onto skewers.
2. Cook the rice.
3. Warm the peanut butter, coconut milk, remaining sugar and soy sauce and the lime juice, stirring until smooth.
4. Grill the skewers for 8 minutes, turning. Serve with the sauce, rice and cucumber.
