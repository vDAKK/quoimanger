# Khao soi | Khao soi (nouilles au curry de Chiang Mai) | 泰北咖喱面
@ Chicken | Thailand | 45 | en:Khao soi | Curry,Soup
- Chicken Legs: 4
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 3 tbs
- Curry Powder: 1 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 800ml
- Chicken Stock: 400ml
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 1 tbs
- Egg Noodles: 400g
- Shallots: 4
- Lime: 2
- Coriander: 1 bunch
1. Fry the curry paste and curry powder in a few spoons of the thick coconut cream until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken, the rest of the coconut milk and the stock and simmer for 30 minutes.
3. Season with fish sauce and palm sugar.
4. Fry a handful of the noodles until crisp; boil the rest.
5. Serve the noodles in the curry, topped with crispy noodles, sliced shallots, lime and coriander.

# Kaeng som | Kaeng som (curry aigre au poisson et légumes) | 泰式酸咖喱
@ Seafood | Thailand | 30 | en:Kaeng som | Curry,Soup
- White Fish Fillets: 500g
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 2 tbs
- Tamarind Paste: 3 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 1 tbs
- Green Papaya: 200g
- Chinese Long Beans: 150g
- Cabbage: 200g
- Water: 1l
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice. Bring the water to the boil and stir in the curry paste.
2. Add the papaya and cabbage and simmer for 8 minutes.
3. Add the beans and the fish in pieces and cook for 4 minutes.
4. Season with tamarind, fish sauce and sugar: it should be sour, salty and spicy.

# Jungle curry | Kaeng pa (curry de la jungle sans coco) | 丛林咖喱
@ Pork | Thailand | 30 | en:Kaeng pa | Curry
- Pork Tenderloin: 400g
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 3 tbs
- Aubergine: 1
- Chinese Long Beans: 150g
- Bamboo Shoot: 150g
- Kaffir Lime Leaves: 4
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Chicken Stock: 600ml
- Vegetable Oil: 2 tbs
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice. Fry the curry paste in the oil.
2. Add the sliced pork and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
3. Add the stock, aubergine, beans, bamboo shoots and lime leaves and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Season with fish sauce and stir in the basil.

# Kaeng hang le | Kaeng hang le (curry de porc du nord) | 泰北猪肉咖喱
@ Pork | Thailand | 120 | en:Kaeng hang le | Curry,Family
- Pork Belly Slices: 800g
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 3 tbs
- Curry Powder: 1 tbs
- Tamarind Paste: 3 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 2 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Ginger: 40g
- Garlic: 8 cloves
- Shallots: 6
- Peanuts: 50g
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Marinate the pork in large cubes with the curry paste and curry powder for 30 minutes.
2. Fry the pork until browned, add 700ml water and simmer for 1 hour.
3. Add the ginger in matchsticks, whole garlic, shallots, tamarind, sugar and fish sauce.
4. Simmer for 30 minutes more until rich and tender. Add the peanuts and serve with rice.

# Som tam | Som tam (salade de papaye verte) | 青木瓜沙拉
@ Seafood | Thailand | 20 | en:Som tam | Quick,Starter
- Green Papaya: 1
- Cherry Tomatoes: 150g
- Chinese Long Beans: 100g
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Birds-eye Chillies: 3
- Dried Shrimp: 2 tbs
- Peanuts: 50g
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Lime: 2
- Palm Sugar: 1 tbs
- Glutinous Rice: 300g
1. Soak and steam the glutinous rice, or cook jasmine rice.
2. Shred the papaya into long thin strips.
3. Pound the garlic and chillies, then the dried shrimp, peanuts and beans.
4. Add the tomatoes, lime juice, fish sauce and sugar and pound lightly.
5. Toss with the papaya and serve with the rice.

# Nam tok beef salad | Nam tok (salade de bœuf grillé) | 泰式烤牛肉沙拉
@ Beef | Thailand | 30 | en:Nam tok (food) | Quick
- Sirloin Steak: 500g
- Glutinous Rice: 3 tbs
- Shallots: 4
- Mint: 1 handful
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Spring Onions: 3
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Lime: 2
- Chilli Flakes: 1 tsp
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
- Cucumber: 1
1. Toast the raw glutinous rice in a dry pan until golden and grind it to a powder.
2. Grill the steak to medium-rare, rest it and slice thinly.
3. Toss the beef with the fish sauce, lime juice, chilli flakes and toasted rice.
4. Add the sliced shallots, herbs and spring onions. Serve with rice and cucumber.

# Moo ping | Moo ping (brochettes de porc grillé) | 泰式烤猪肉串
@ Pork | Thailand | 40 | en:Mu ping
- Pork Shoulder: 700g
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- White Pepper: 1 tsp
- Oyster Sauce: 2 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 1 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 1 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 2 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 100ml
- Glutinous Rice: 300g
- Cucumber: 1
1. Pound the garlic, coriander stems and white pepper, then mix with the sauces and sugar.
2. Slice the pork thinly and marinate for at least 1 hour.
3. Thread onto skewers.
4. Grill, brushing with coconut milk, for 10 minutes until caramelised.
5. Serve with sticky rice and cucumber.

# Gai yang | Gai yang (poulet grillé à la thaïe) | 泰式烤鸡
@ Chicken | Thailand | 60 | en:Kai yang
- Chicken Legs: 6
- Garlic: 6 cloves
- Coriander: 1 bunch
- Lemongrass: 2
- Fish Sauce: 3 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Palm Sugar: 1 tbs
- White Pepper: 1 tsp
- Sweet Chilli Sauce: 5 tbs
- Glutinous Rice: 300g
- Cucumber: 1
1. Blend the garlic, coriander stems, lemongrass, sauces, sugar and pepper.
2. Marinate the chicken for at least 2 hours.
3. Grill or roast at 200°C for 40 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through.
4. Serve with sweet chilli sauce, sticky rice and cucumber.

# Rad na | Rad na (nouilles larges en sauce) | 泰式滑蛋河粉
@ Pork | Thailand | 25 | en:Rat na | Quick
- Flat Rice Noodles: 400g
- Pork Tenderloin: 300g
- Chinese Broccoli: 300g
- Dark Soy Sauce: 2 tbs
- Oyster Sauce: 2 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 1 tbs
- Sugar: 1 tsp
- Chicken Stock: 500ml
- Cornstarch: 2 tbs
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- White Vinegar: 2 tbs
1. Stir-fry the noodles with the dark soy sauce until slightly charred and divide between plates.
2. Fry the garlic and sliced pork, then the Chinese broccoli.
3. Add the stock, oyster sauce, soy sauce and sugar, then thicken with the cornstarch.

# Khanom chin nam ngiao | Khanom chin nam ngiao (vermicelles, porc et tomates) | 泰北番茄猪肉米线

- Thai Red Curry Paste: 2 tbs
- Shrimp Paste: 1 tsp
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Bean Sprouts: 150g
- Cabbage: 150g

# Hor mok | Hor mok (flan de poisson au curry vapeur) | 泰式蒸鱼糕
@ Seafood | Thailand | 45 | en:Hor mok
- White Fish Fillets: 500g
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 2 tbs
- Coconut Milk: 300ml
- Eggs: 2
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Kaffir Lime Leaves: 4
- Basil Leaves: 1 handful
- Cabbage: 150g
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Cook the rice. Cut the fish into small pieces.
2. Whisk the curry paste, 250ml coconut milk, eggs and fish sauce, then add the fish.
3. Line small bowls with basil and shredded cabbage and fill with the mixture.
4. Steam for 15 minutes, top with the remaining coconut milk and shredded lime leaves and steam for 2 minutes.

- Coriander: 1 bunch

# Pork satay with peanut sauce | Saté de porc sauce cacahuète | 沙爹猪肉串
@ Pork | Thailand | 45 | en:Satay
- Pork Tenderloin: 700g
- Coconut Milk: 400ml
- Curry Powder: 2 tsp
- Ground Turmeric: 1 tsp
- Palm Sugar: 2 tbs
- Fish Sauce: 2 tbs
- Peanut Butter: 4 tbs
- Thai Red Curry Paste: 1 tbs
- Cucumber: 1
- Red Onions: 1
- White Vinegar: 3 tbs
- Jasmine Rice: 300g
1. Marinate the thin strips of pork with 100ml coconut milk, curry powder, turmeric, half the sugar and fish sauce.
2. Simmer the remaining coconut milk with the curry paste, peanut butter and remaining sugar until thick.
3. Thread the pork onto skewers and grill for 8 minutes.
4. Mix the diced cucumber and onion with the vinegar and a pinch of sugar.
5. Serve the skewers with peanut sauce, cucumber relish and rice.
