# Bresse-style chicken in cream | Poulet à la crème façon Bresse | 布雷斯奶油鸡
@ Chicken | France | 60 | File:Poulet de Bresse à la crème, Le Café Français (Bourg-en-Bresse), janvier 2024.jpg | Classic,Family
- Chicken Legs: 6
- Mushrooms: 250g
- Shallots: 3
- Dry White Wine: 200ml
- Chicken Stock: 200ml
- Creme Fraiche: 300ml
- Butter: 40g
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Thyme: 2 sprigs
- Rice: 300g
1. Brown the chicken pieces in butter. Add the shallots, then sprinkle with flour and stir.
2. Add the wine, stock and thyme. Cover and simmer for 35 minutes.
3. Sauté the mushrooms separately and add them to the pot.
4. Remove the chicken, stir the crème fraîche into the sauce and reduce for 5 minutes. Serve with rice.

# Lyonnaise salad | Salade lyonnaise | 里昂沙拉
@ Pork | France | 25 | fr:Salade lyonnaise | Starter,Salad,Quick,Eggs
- Little Gem Lettuce: 3
- Bacon Lardon: 200g
- Eggs: 4
- Bread: 4 slices
- Garlic: 1 clove
- Red Wine Vinegar: 3 tbs
- Dijon Mustard: 1 tsp
- Olive Oil: 4 tbs
- Shallots: 1
1. Fry the bacon until crisp. Toast cubes of bread rubbed with garlic in the bacon fat.
2. Whisk the mustard, vinegar, oil and chopped shallot.
3. Poach the eggs for 3 minutes in simmering water with a splash of vinegar.
4. Toss the salad with the dressing, bacon and croutons and top each plate with an egg.

# Lyonnaise potatoes with sausages | Pommes de terre lyonnaises et saucisses | 里昂土豆配香肠
@ Pork | France | 40 | fr:Pommes de terre à la lyonnaise | Family
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Onions: 3
- Sausages: 8
- Butter: 50g
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Lettuce: 1
1. Boil the potatoes in their skins for 15 minutes, then peel and slice.
2. Cook the sliced onions slowly in half the butter until golden.
3. Fry the potatoes in the rest of the butter until crisp, then mix with the onions.
4. Pan-fry the sausages. Serve with parsley and a green salad.

# Basque veal axoa | Axoa de veau | 巴斯克辣椒炖小牛肉

- Chilli Flakes: 0.5 tsp

# Garbure | Garbure | 加斯科涅蔬菜浓汤
@ Pork | France | 120 | fr:Garbure | Soup,Classic

- Savoy Cabbage: 0.5
- Haricot Beans: 400g
- Potatoes: 3
- Carrots: 3
- Turnips: 2
- Leek: 1
- Onions: 1

- Crusty Bread: 1

# Duck breast with sarladaise potatoes | Magret de canard et pommes sarladaises | 鸭胸配萨尔拉土豆
@ Miscellaneous | France | 45 | fr:Pommes de terre sarladaises | Classic
- Duck Breast: 2
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Parsley: 1 bunch
- Green Beans: 300g
- Honey: 1 tbs
- Balsamic Vinegar: 2 tbs
1. Score the duck skin and cook skin-side down in a cold pan over medium heat for 12 minutes. Pour off and keep the fat.
2. Turn and cook 4 minutes. Rest 10 minutes, then slice.
3. Fry the sliced potatoes in duck fat for 20 minutes. Add the chopped garlic and parsley at the end.
4. Deglaze the pan with honey and vinegar. Serve with green beans.

- Gruyere: 120g
- Nutmeg: 1 pinch
- Lemon: 0.5
