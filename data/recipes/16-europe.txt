# Pork schnitzel with potato salad | Escalope viennoise et salade de pommes de terre | 炸猪排配土豆沙拉
@ Pork | Austria | 45 | en:Wiener schnitzel | Classic,Fried,Family
- Pork Tenderloin: 600g
- Plain Flour: 60g
- Eggs: 2
- Breadcrumbs: 150g
- Potatoes: 800g
- Onions: 1
- White Wine Vinegar: 3 tbs
- Beef Stock: 150ml
- Dijon Mustard: 1 tsp
- Chives: 1 bunch
- Lemon: 1
- Sunflower Oil: 200ml
1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel and slice while warm.
2. Heat the stock with the finely chopped onion, vinegar, mustard, salt and a little oil. Pour over the potatoes and leave for 20 minutes. Add the chives.
3. Cut the pork into escalopes and flatten them thinly. Coat in flour, beaten egg, then breadcrumbs.
4. Fry in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes per side. Serve with lemon wedges and the salad.

# Hungarian goulash | Goulasch hongrois | 匈牙利牛肉汤
@ Beef | Hungary | 120 | en:Goulash | Soup,Stew,Classic
- Beef Shin: 800g
- Onions: 3
- Sweet Smoked Paprika: 3 tbs
- Caraway Seed: 1 tsp
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Tomatoes: 2
- Red Pepper: 2
- Potatoes: 4
- Carrots: 2
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Lard: 2 tbs
- Crusty Bread: 1
1. Fry the chopped onions in lard until golden.
2. Off the heat, stir in the paprika, then add the beef cubes, caraway and garlic.
3. Add 1.5 litres of water and the tomato puree. Simmer covered for 1 h 15.
4. Add the potatoes, carrots, peppers and tomatoes and cook for 30 minutes. Serve with bread.

# Chicken paprikash | Poulet au paprika | 匈牙利红椒鸡
@ Chicken | Hungary | 60 | en:Chicken paprikash | Family
- Chicken Thighs: 8
- Onions: 2
- Sweet Smoked Paprika: 2 tbs
- Red Pepper: 1
- Tomatoes: 2
- Chicken Stock: 400ml
- Sour Cream: 200ml
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Butter: 30g
- Egg Noodles: 400g
1. Brown the chicken in butter and set aside. Soften the onions in the same pan.
2. Off the heat add the paprika, then the pepper, tomatoes, chicken and stock.
3. Simmer for 35 minutes.
4. Mix the sour cream with the flour and stir into the sauce. Heat without boiling. Serve with noodles.

# German beef rouladen | Paupiettes de bœuf allemandes | 德式牛肉卷
@ Beef | Germany | 120 | en:Rouladen | Braise,Classic
- Beef Cutlet: 8
- Streaky Bacon: 8 slices
- Dijon Mustard: 4 tbs
- Dill Pickles: 4
- Onions: 2
- Beef Stock: 500ml
- Red Wine: 200ml
- Carrots: 1
- Tomato Puree: 1 tbs
- Potatoes: 1kg
1. Flatten the beef slices, spread with mustard and top with bacon, onion and a pickle quarter.
2. Roll up and secure with toothpicks. Brown well in a casserole.
3. Add the chopped carrot and remaining onion, then the tomato puree, wine and stock.
4. Simmer covered for 1 h 30. Thicken the sauce and serve with boiled potatoes.

# Kaesespaetzle | Käsespätzle (pâtes fraîches au fromage) | 德国奶酪面疙瘩
@ Vegetarian | Germany | 40 | en:Käsespätzle | Cheese,Family
- Plain Flour: 400g
- Eggs: 4
- Milk: 150ml
- Emmentaler Cheese: 250g
- Onions: 3
- Butter: 50g
- Chives: 1 bunch
- Rocket: 100g
1. Beat the flour, eggs, milk and salt into a thick, sticky batter.
2. Press or scrape the batter into boiling salted water. Lift out the spätzle when they float.
3. Fry the sliced onions in butter until crisp.
4. Layer the spätzle with the grated cheese in a hot dish. Top with onions and chives. Serve with a green salad.

# Zurich-style veal | Émincé de veau à la zurichoise | 苏黎世奶油小牛肉
@ Beef | Switzerland | 30 | en:Zürcher Geschnetzeltes | Quick,Classic
- Veal: 600g
- Mushrooms: 250g
- Shallots: 2
- Dry White Wine: 150ml
- Double Cream: 250ml
- Beef Stock: 100ml
- Butter: 30g
- Plain Flour: 1 tbs
- Parsley: 1 handful
- Potatoes: 800g
1. Cut the veal into thin strips, dust with flour and brown quickly in butter. Set aside.
2. Fry the shallots and sliced mushrooms.
3. Add the wine and reduce, then the stock and cream. Simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Return the veal to warm through. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with rösti or potatoes.

# Swedish meatballs | Boulettes suédoises | 瑞典肉丸
@ Beef | Sweden | 45 | en:Swedish meatballs | Family,Classic
- Minced Beef: 400g
- Minced Pork: 200g
- Breadcrumbs: 50g
- Milk: 100ml
- Onions: 1
- Eggs: 1
- Ground Allspice: 0.5 tsp
- Butter: 40g
- Plain Flour: 2 tbs
- Beef Stock: 400ml
- Double Cream: 150ml
- Potatoes: 1kg
- Cranberry: 4 tbs

2. Roll into small balls and fry in butter until browned. Set aside.

# Danish pork with parsley sauce | Porc croustillant sauce persil | 丹麦脆猪肉配欧芹酱

- Potatoes: 1kg
- Butter: 40g
- Plain Flour: 3 tbs
- Milk: 500ml
- Parsley: 2 bunches
- Nutmeg: 1 pinch
1. Fry the pork slices slowly in a dry pan until very crisp, about 20 minutes.

# Czech svickova | Svíčková (bœuf sauce crème aux légumes) | 捷克奶油酱牛肉
@ Beef | Czech Republic | 150 | en:Svíčková | Classic,Braise
- Beef: 1kg
- Carrots: 3
- Celeriac: 0.5
- Parsnips: 2
- Onions: 2
- Bacon: 100g
- Bay Leaves: 3
- Allspice Berries: 6
- Lemon: 1
- Double Cream: 250ml
- Cranberry: 4 tbs
- Bread: 8 slices
1. Lard the beef with bacon strips. Brown it with the diced vegetables and onions.
2. Add the spices and 500ml water and braise covered at 170°C for 2 hours.
3. Remove the meat and blend the vegetables with the juices, cream and lemon juice until smooth.
4. Serve the sliced beef with the sauce, cranberry and slices of bread or dumplings.

# Czech roast pork with dumplings and cabbage | Porc rôti, knödel et chou | 捷克烤猪肉配团子和酸菜
@ Pork | Czech Republic | 120 | en:Vepřo knedlo zelo | Roast,Classic
- Pork Shoulder: 1.2kg
- Caraway Seed: 2 tsp
- Garlic: 4 cloves
- Onions: 2
- Red Cabbage: 1
- Sugar: 2 tbs
- Cider Vinegar: 3 tbs
- Bread: 6 slices
- Plain Flour: 300g
- Milk: 200ml
- Eggs: 1
- Instant Yeast: 7g
1. Rub the pork with garlic, caraway and salt. Roast on sliced onions at 180°C for 1 h 45, basting with water.
2. Braise the shredded red cabbage with sugar, vinegar and a little water for 45 minutes.
3. Mix flour, milk, egg, yeast, salt and the diced bread. Rise 30 minutes, shape into 2 logs and steam or simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Slice and serve with the pork, juices and cabbage.
