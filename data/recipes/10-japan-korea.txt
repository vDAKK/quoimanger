# Tonjiru | Tonjiru (soupe miso au porc et légumes) | 猪肉味噌汤
@ Pork | Japan | 35 | en:Tonjiru | Soup
- Pork Belly Slices: 250g
- Daikon Radish: 200g
- Carrots: 1
- Potatoes: 2
- Onion: 1
- Tofu: 200g
- Miso: 4 tbs
- Dashi: 2 tsp
- Spring Onions: 2
- Sushi Rice: 320g
1. Cook the rice. Fry the pork pieces in a pot until they colour.
2. Add the sliced radish, carrot, potatoes and onion and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Add 1.2 litres of water and the dashi and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Add the tofu, then dissolve the miso in the soup off the boil. Sprinkle with spring onions.

# Miso soup | Soupe miso | 味噌汤
@ Vegetarian | Japan | 10 | en:Miso soup | Quick,Soup,Side

3. Off the heat, dissolve the miso through a small sieve.
4. Serve with sliced spring onions.

# Omurice | Omurice (omelette au riz ketchup) | 蛋包饭

- Onion: 1
- Frozen Peas: 80g
- Tomato Ketchup: 6 tbs
- Eggs: 8
- Butter: 40g
- Milk: 4 tbs
1. Cook the rice. Fry the diced chicken and onion in half the butter.
2. Add the rice, peas and 4 tbs ketchup and toss until red.

# Tamagoyaki | Tamagoyaki (omelette roulée japonaise) | 玉子烧
@ Vegetarian | Japan | 15 | en:Tamagoyaki | Quick,Side
- Eggs: 6
- Sugar: 1 tbs
- Soy Sauce: 1 tsp
- Mirin: 1 tbs
- Dashi: 1/2 tsp
- Vegetable Oil: 2 tbs
1. Beat the eggs with the sugar, soy sauce, mirin, dashi and 3 tbs water.
2. Pour a thin layer into an oiled pan, let it set and roll it to one side.
3. Pour in another layer, lifting the roll so the egg runs under it, then roll back.
4. Repeat until the egg is used up. Rest for 2 minutes and slice.

# Tonkotsu ramen | Ramen tonkotsu (bouillon de porc crémeux) | 豚骨拉面
@ Pork | Japan | 240 | en:Tonkotsu ramen | Soup
- Pigs Trotters: 2
- Pork Belly Slices: 500g
- Garlic: 1 bulb
- Ginger: 40g
- Onion: 1
- Soy Sauce: 6 tbs
- Mirin: 3 tbs
- Eggs: 4
- Egg Noodles: 400g
- Spring Onions: 3
- Nori: 2
- Bean Sprouts: 150g
1. Blanch the trotters, rinse, then boil hard in 3 litres of water with the garlic, ginger and onion for 3 hours, topping up with water, until milky.
2. Roll the pork belly, simmer it in the soy sauce, mirin and 300ml water for 1 hour 30, turning, then slice.
3. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes 30, peel and soak them in the pork braising liquid.
4. Strain the broth and season with some of the braising liquid.
5. Cook the noodles and serve in the broth with the pork, halved eggs, bean sprouts, nori and spring onions.
